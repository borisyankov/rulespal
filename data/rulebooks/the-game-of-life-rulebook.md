# The Game of Life Rulebook

## Table of Contents

*The Game of Life is the classic family board game where players spin the wheel and travel through a lifetime of careers, marriage, children, houses, and retirement. For 2 to 6 players. Object: Collect money and LIFE Tiles, and have the highest dollar amount at the end of the game. © 1991 Milton Bradley Company.*

## Setting Up

- Attach the game parts to the gameboard as shown on the Assembly Sheet.
- **LIFE Tiles:** Each carries a secret message (a special achievement) and a dollar amount counting toward your total. Mix them LIFE-side-up near the board; draw 4 tiles and, without looking at the messages, stack them LIFE-side-up at **Millionaire Estates**. The rest form the draw pile.
- **Cards:** Separate into 4 decks—Career Cards, Salary Cards, House Deeds, and Stocks—placed facedown near the board.
- **Insurance Policies & Bank Loans:** Separate the Automobile Insurance Policies, Homeowner's Insurance Policies, and Bank Loans into piles.
- **Money:** Choose a banker (in charge of all money paid to and from the bank). The banker gives each player **$10,000**.
- **Cars and Pegs:** Choose a car and fit a people peg into the driver's seat.

## Game Play

All players spin the wheel; the highest spinner takes the first turn, then play continues clockwise.

**On your turn,** spin the wheel (spin again if it stops between numbers), then move your car that many spaces. Always move **forward**, in the direction of the arrows. If you land on an occupied space, move ahead to the next open space. Follow the space directions, which ends your turn. *(Your first turn is an exception—see Getting Started.)*

## Getting Started

On your first turn, decide **either** to start a career **or** to start college (college offers more career and salary options but takes time and puts you in debt).

- **Start Career:** Place your car on the START CAREER space, then: **Draw a Career Card** (have another player fan out the deck while you draw 1 at random; you may not keep a "Degree Required" card—if you draw one, draw again). **Draw a Salary Card** (draw 1). Place both faceup in front of you, then spin and move as on a regular turn.
- **Start College:** Place your car on the START COLLEGE space and borrow **$40,000** from the bank for tuition (see Bank Loans). Then spin and move as on a regular turn. When you later reach **Job Search**, choose your Career and Salary cards.

**Job Search:** When you reach this space, stop (even with moves left). Have another player fan out the Career Card deck facedown while you draw **3** cards at random; choose 1 and return the other 2. Choose your Salary Card the same way. Place both faceup in front of you, then spin and move again.

## The Spaces

**Space colors:**
- **Orange:** most spaces—you **must** follow the directions.
- **Blue:** optional—follow the directions only if you want to.
- **Green (Pay Day):** whenever you land on or pass, collect your salary from the bank.
- **Red:** the 3 red spaces are Job Search, Get Married, and Buy a House—stop (even with moves left), follow the directions, then spin and move again.

**LIFE spaces:** Whenever you land on a LIFE space, take 1 LIFE Tile from the draw pile (or from any opponent if the pile is empty). Do not look at the back; place it LIFE-side-up in front of you.

**Career Spaces:** The symbols match those on the 9 Career Cards. When you land on a Career Space: if an **opponent** owns the matching Career Card, pay that player the amount on the space; if **you** own it, pay nothing; if **no player** owns it, pay the bank. (Example: the Ski Accident space is a Doctor's Career Space.)

**Taxes Due Spaces:** The Accountant's Career Spaces—pay the Accountant the taxes on your Salary Card (if you are the Accountant, pay nothing; if there is none, pay the bank).

**Special Police Officer Rule:** Any opponent caught speeding (spinning 10) must pay the Police Officer $5,000. If you're the Police Officer, it's up to you to notice; if there's no Police Officer, there's no penalty for spinning 10.

**Changing Careers:** On the "You're Fired" and "Mid-Life Crisis" spaces, you **must** trade your Career and Salary cards for new ones (draw 1 Career Card; you may not keep a "Degree Required" card if you're not a college graduate). On **Night School** spaces, you **may** change careers and salaries: draw 2 Career Cards and choose either (or keep your own), then do the same with Salary Cards.

**Pay Day:** Whenever you land on or pass a Pay Day space, collect your salary from the bank before following any other directions (you may collect 2+ salaries on some turns).

**Getting Married:** Stop (even with moves left). Take a LIFE Tile and add 1 people peg to your car, then spin and move again.

**Babies:** On a Baby Boy or Baby Girl space, add 1 people peg; on a Twins space, add 2 pegs. On all of these, take a LIFE Tile too.

**Buying a House:** Stop (even with moves left). Draw 1 House Deed at random and pay the bank the price on it (borrow from the bank if short), then spin and move again.

## Insurance, Stocks & Loans

- **Automobile Insurance:** Buy at the start of any of your turns—pay $10,000 and take 1 policy—protecting your car against accidents, damage, and theft.
- **Homeowner's Insurance:** Buy when you buy a house (or at the start of a later turn)—pay the insurance amount on your House Deed and take 1 policy—protecting against floods, tornadoes, and theft.
- **Stocks:** Buy 1 Stock at the start of any turn (pay $50,000, take 1 Stock of your choice). Whenever any player spins the number on your Stock, collect $10,000. You can own only 1 Stock (exceptions: the Stock Market Zooms space gives 1 free Stock; you can re-buy after losing your only Stock on a Stock Market Slumps space).
- **Bank Loans:** Borrow during any turn—take 1 or more loans, each worth $20,000. Repay all loans (with $5,000 interest each) when you retire or during any turn before then.

## Retirement

When you reach the RETIRE space, stop (even with moves left), then:
- Repay any outstanding loans to the bank.
- Place your Career Card, Salary Card, Insurance Policies, and House Deed out of play (keep your Stock—you can still collect when players spin your number).
- If you think you might have the most money, place your car on **Millionaire Estates** (any number may retire there); otherwise place it on **Countryside Acres**.
- Wait there until all other players have retired.

If you retire at **Millionaire Estates**, your LIFE tiles are **not** safe—if the draw pile runs out, players may take tiles from you when they land on LIFE spaces. If you retire at **Countryside Acres**, collect 1 LIFE Tile, and your tiles are **safe**.

## How to Win

After all players have retired, the players at **Millionaire Estates** count their money; the richest takes the 4 LIFE Tiles there (a tie splits them, 2 tiles each). Then all players turn their LIFE Tiles message-side-up and add up the dollar amounts, count their money, and add the two figures together (LIFE Tile value plus cash value). The player with the highest dollar amount wins!
