# Simon Rulebook

## Table of Contents

**Object:** Watch, remember, repeat.

## The First Time You Play

1. Replace the demo batteries with alkaline batteries. Use a Phillips/cross head screwdriver (not included). The unit requires 3 x 1.5V AAA batteries.
2. Press the power button to turn the unit **ON**.
3. While the unit is on, press the power button to adjust the **VOLUME** (loud, medium, quiet, mute).
4. Press and hold the power button to see the **HIGH SCORE**.
5. The unit turns off after 15 seconds of inactivity.

## Play

1. Press the flashing green light to start.
2. A color will flash. Press that color to repeat the sequence.
3. The unit will add another color to the sequence. Repeat the sequence to get another color added, and so on.
4. When you don't complete a sequence successfully, the unit will play a fail sound and display your score.

## Scoring

When you see | You've earned
--- | ---
All lights flash | 10 points
Green light flash | 1 point

## Win

Complete the longest sequence to get the new high score!

## Note

If the unit is not working properly, try replacing the batteries or pushing in the reset button on the back (use a paper clip if needed). The high score will be lost when you replace batteries or reset.
