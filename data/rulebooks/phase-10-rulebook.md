# Phase 10 Rulebook

## Table of Contents

*Phase 10 is played over a series of rounds in which players race to build ten specific card combinations, called "Phases," in order. Complete your Phase and shed your remaining cards to advance; fall short and you must attempt the same Phase again next round. The first player to finish all ten Phases wins. For 2 to 6 players.*

## Object

Be the first player to complete all **10 Phases**, in order.

## Components

- **108 cards:** 96 numbered cards (two sets of 1–12 in each of green, yellow, red, and orange), 8 **Wild** cards, and 4 **Skip** cards.
- **6 Phase Reference Boards** (listing the ten Phases) and **6 Phase Trackers** (pointers).

## The Ten Phases

Phases must be completed **in order**, from 1 to 10:

1. 2 sets of 3
2. 1 set of 3 + 1 run of 4
3. 1 set of 4 + 1 run of 4
4. 1 run of 7
5. 1 run of 8
6. 1 run of 9
7. 2 sets of 4
8. 7 cards of one color
9. 1 set of 5 + 1 set of 2
10. 1 set of 5 + 1 set of 3

**Definitions:**
- **Set:** two or more cards of the same number, in any mix of colors.
- **Run:** four or more cards in consecutive number order, in any mix of colors.
- **All one color:** cards all sharing one color, not necessarily in number order.

## Setup

1. Choose a first dealer. Give each player one Phase Reference Board and one Phase Tracker; each player sets their tracker to point at **Phase 1**. Advance the tracker each time you complete a Phase.
2. The dealer shuffles and deals **10 cards facedown** to each player. Keep your hand hidden.
3. Place the remaining deck facedown as the **draw pile**, then turn its top card faceup beside it to start the **discard pile**.
4. The player to the dealer's left goes first; play proceeds clockwise.

## A Turn

Each turn has four steps:

**1. Draw a card** — take the top card of either the draw pile or the discard pile into your hand.

**2. Lay down a Phase (if able)** — if your hand contains the current Phase, lay it faceup on the table. You may complete **only one Phase per round**, and you must hold the entire Phase before laying it down. In the first round everyone works on Phase 1. You get credit for a Phase the moment you lay it down—you do not have to go out to advance.

**3. Hit (if able)** — once your own Phase is down, you may "hit": play cards from your hand directly onto any laid-down Phase that they fit (your own or another player's). For example, add more matching numbers to a set, extend a run at either end, or add matching-color/Wild cards to a color Phase. You may make as many hits as you can on your turn. Hitting is how you get rid of your remaining cards.

**4. Discard** — end your turn by discarding one card faceup onto the discard pile.

## Special Cards

**Wild card** — stands for any number and any color to help complete or hit a Phase. You may use as many Wilds as you like as long as at least one numbered card is also used. Once played into a Phase, a Wild cannot be moved for the rest of the round. If the dealer flips a Wild to start the discard pile, the first player may take it.

**Skip card** — its only use is to make another player lose a turn. On your turn, discard a Skip by placing it in front of a chosen player instead of on the discard pile; that player is skipped next turn (their only action then is to move the Skip onto the discard pile). Playing a Skip counts as your discard and ends your turn. A Skip may never be drawn from the discard pile, never used in a Phase, and never played on a player who already has a Skip in front of them. If the dealer starts the discard pile with a Skip, the first player's turn is skipped.

## Going Out and Ending the Round

After laying down a Phase, players try to **go out**—get rid of all remaining cards by hitting and/or discarding the last card. The first player to go out wins the round, ending it for everyone.

Every player who **completed their Phase** this round (the winner and any others) advances their Phase Tracker to the next Phase. Anyone who did **not** complete their Phase must attempt the **same Phase** again next round—so players may be on different Phases.

## Scoring

The round winner scores **0**. Each other player scores penalty points for cards still in hand (lower is better):
- **5** points per card numbered 1–9
- **10** points per card numbered 10–12
- **15** points per Skip card
- **25** points per Wild card

Only cards still in hand are scored, not cards laid down. Record scores, pass the deal to the left, reshuffle, and begin a new round.

## Winning

The first player to complete **Phase 10** at the end of a round wins. If two or more finish Phase 10 in the same round, the one with the **fewest total points** wins. If still tied, those players replay Phase 10 and the first to go out wins.

## Variations

- **Ten rounds:** every player advances a Phase each round regardless of completion; after ten rounds, lowest total score wins.
- **Fewer Phases:** agree before play on how many Phases are needed to win (e.g. five or seven) for a shorter game; all other rules stand.
