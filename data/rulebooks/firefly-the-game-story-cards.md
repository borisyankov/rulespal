# Firefly: The Game Story Cards

## Table of Contents

*Each game of Firefly starts with choosing a Story Card. Story Cards detail what each player needs to accomplish to win the game. Story Cards tell different tales. Sometimes players will compete to be the first to pull off a daring heist. Other Story Cards tell the tale of daring desperados hoping to escape Alliance "justice". Each Story Card requires a different strategy; adapting to different goals is the mark of a great captain! Collected here are the official bonus Story Cards released by Gale Force Nine for Firefly: The Game.*

## First Time in the Captain's Chair

*1-2 Hours — Recommended for New Players*

*So you finally took the plunge and borrowed enough credits for a ship to call your own. You're the master of your destiny and right now that destiny looks mighty uncertain! You're in debt up to your eyeballs with a creditor that's not the sort of man to be trifled with.*

### Set-Up

When taking Starting Jobs, only take a Job from Harken and Amnon Duul. These Jobs may be discarded, as normal.

### Goal 1: Makin' Friends

*You ain't gonna get far in the 'Verse without makin' a few friends. It's just good business.*

To Complete Goal, be Solid with two different Contacts. After completing this Goal, take a Goal Token.

### Goal 2: Seein' Daylight

*Carrying a heap a' debt on your back is no way to live. Scrape together enough cash to get back in the black.*

To Complete Goal, have $6000 in hand and your first Goal Token. After completing this Goal, take a second Goal Token.

### Goal 3: Feeding the Shark

*Location: Ezra, Georgia*

*Head over to pay off your loan shark. Adelai Niska is not a patient man...*

The first player who travels to Ezra with two Goal Tokens and then pays the bank $6000 wins the game. You own your Ship free and clear!

## Niska's Holiday

*2 Hours*

*Adelia Niska is taking a holiday and has left his operations in the incompetent hands of one of his wife's many nephews. This presents an opportunity for an ambitious Captain to prove himself during his absence. Insure the continuing profitability of Niska's criminal enterprise and ensure his nephew's failure.*

### Goal 1: Sabotage the Competition

*Location: Aesir, Himinbjorg — Misbehave x3*

*Niska's nephew has a crew pulling a heist on Aesir. See to it that it does not go according to plan.*

Fight Test 10:

* 1-9: Kill 2 Crew. Attempt Botched & Warrant Issued.
* 10+: Success: Goal Complete.

### Goal 2: By Their Works, Ye Shall Know Them…

*Prove you're a Captain who gets things done by taking care of business during Niska's absence.*

To Complete Goal, successfuly complete 4 Niska Jobs and be Solid with Niska.

### Goal 3: Return of the King

*Location: Ezra, Georgia*

*Welcome Niska back from his much needed vacation. Report on his nephew's failures and deliver the profits you've collected in his absence.*

Must be Solid with Niska. Pay $12,000 - You Win! Niska names you one of his lieutenants. You are now a "made" man.
