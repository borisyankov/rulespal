## The Survivors

*by Croc*

*Our numbers, our faith and our courage combined were not enough to avoid disaster. The cleansing of the underground tunnels has turned into a bloodbath sooner than we expected: great numbers of Troglodytes, roaming the tunnels in packs, backed up by Demons, have scattered the mortals sent below before feasting on the isolated ones. Panic has ruined the hopes of the expedition. Only a handful has managed to survive, guided by the Redeemer. As they wander, lost in the labyrinth, they must try to find their way out, even if this means helping themselves to loot from the bodies of their slaughtered companions.*

*But their enemies grow ever nearer, and although they have already feasted on Human flesh, they are still hungry.*

### Components to prepare

**Human player**

- Leader: The REDEEMER with the gifts AURA OF COURAGE and PRESCIENCE.
- Fighters: CONDEMNED SCOUT equipped with a BLUNDERBUSS; CONDEMNED SCOUT; CONDEMNED BRUTE.

**Infernal player**

- Demons: During the game, the HUNTER can be brought into play.
- Hellhounds (x2)
- Troglodytes (x11)
- Prima matiera: Starting TP of 4.

DIRECTION token, TURN marker.

### Scenario setup

1. Remove the EXIT tile (27) and the PENTACLE tile (26) from the A pack of tiles and set them aside.
2. Shuffle the other tiles and form a B pile.
3. The PENTACLE tile (26) is placed in the center of the table and the Western warriors are placed upon it.

### Victory conditions

- The human player wins if they manage to reach the EXIT tile (27) with at least 2 of their warriors still alive.
- The infernal player wins if they manage to kill all of the Western warriors.
- Any other result is a tie.

### Special rules

**Blue skies**

When a Western warrior reaches the EXIT tile (27), they are removed from the game. Demon warriors cannot enter the EXIT tile (27).

**A draft of fresh air**

To find their way out, the Western warriors are following a faint draft of fresh air that leads them towards the exit.

At the start of the game, the infernal player places the DIRECTION token next to one of the unexplored openings of the PENTACLE tile (26). They also place the TURN marker on the first space on the back of the EXIT tile (27).

When the corresponding opening is explored, the infernal player moves the TURN marker one space forward and places the DIRECTION token next to an unexplored opening of the newly explored tile. If this one does not have an unexplored opening, the infernal player places the DIRECTION token next to the unexplored opening of the closest tile.

When the TURN marker reaches 10, the next tile is not randomly taken from the pile; the infernal player turns over the EXIT tile (27) over and places it as the final tile.

## Turning the Tide

*by Croc*

*The situation has become worrisome. Continually suffering ever more violent onslaughts, the Humans have not had any time to build proper defenses. There are always demons left to galvanize the hordes of Troglodytes. The outlook was particularly bleak for the hours to come, when the Council of New Jerusalem sent word to the Redeemer of a new discovery. While studying maps of the catacombs, the authorities had come across a pit far deeper than any other they had ever charted. It was obvious that most of the demonic forces were coming from this abyss.*

*From that moment, the objective was no longer to simply hold back the waves of infernal creatures, but to truly stop their troops for a while. The Redeemer and his soldiers have been sent below once again, this time carrying bags of explosives.*

### Components to prepare

**Human player**

- Leader: The REDEEMER with the gifts ASSEMBLY and SACRIFICIAL STRIKE.
- Fighters: a CONDEMNED SCOUT equipped with a GRENADE; a CONDEMNED SCOUT; a CONDEMNED BRUTE equipped with a STEEL SHIELD.

**Infernal player**

- Demons: During the game, the SCOURGE can be brought into play.
- Hellhounds (x2)
- Troglodytes (x11)
- Prima matiera: Starting TP of 4.

### Scenario setup

1. The STASH tiles (20 to 22) and the PENTACLE tile (26) are not used in this game and are put back in the box.
2. Remove the EXIT tile (27) and STASH tile (19) from the A pack of tiles and set them aside.
3. Make a B pile facedown of 12 randomly drawn tiles from the remainder of the A pack.
4. Make a C pile (facedown) by shuffling 3 randomly drawn tiles from the remainder of the A pack with the STASH tile (19).
5. Place the B pile on the C pile. The resulting D pile contains 16 tiles; the STASH tile (19) is among the last 4 tiles.
6. The EXIT tile (27) is placed in the center of the table and the Western warriors are placed upon it.

### Victory conditions

- The human player wins the game if they manage to close the wells leading into the bowels of hell.
- Any other result is a victory for the infernal player.

### Special rules

**Laying explosives**

A Western warrior, who is not exhausted, beginning their activation on the STASH tile (19), uses the explosives and closes the well leading to the bowels of hell.

## The Possessed

*by Croc*

*Worrying reports have reached Brother Marcus. They tell of Condemned warriors wandering silent and red-eyed through the tunnels that run under New Jerusalem. They are said to be warriors who were reported to have lost their lives during previous expeditions. The Council is already reporting that several victims have been devoured by these lost souls. The order came soon after the first reports: Brother Marcus is being sent down with his men to combat this new evil. Inside him burns the desire to free these Condemned ones, whose true nature he has guessed. They are possessed, a mantle of mortal flesh worn by immaterial demons.*

*Such an abomination can only inspire the wrath of the righteous and a total purification.*

### Components to prepare

**Human player**

- Leader: The REDEEMER with the gifts AURA OF COURAGE, ASSEMBLY, and LAYING ON OF HANDS.
- Fighters: a CONDEMNED SCOUT equipped with a BLUNDERBUSS; a CONDEMNED BRUTE.
- Bonus: Two randomly drawn instinct cards.

**Infernal player**

- Demons: During the game, two POSSESSED can be brought into play (see special rules THE MONSTER'S LAIR).
- Hellhounds (x2)
- Troglodytes (x11)
- Prima matiera: Starting TP of 4.

Also prepare: CONDEMNED BRUTE's board and miniature, CONDEMNED SCOUT's board and miniature.

### Scenario setup

1. The STASH tiles (21 & 22) and the PENTACLE tile (26) are not used for this game and are put back in the box.
2. Remove the EXIT tile (27) and the STASH tiles (19 & 20) from the A pack of tiles and set them aside.
3. Make a B pile from 3 randomly drawn tiles drawn from the remainder of the A pack and place them facedown.
4. Make a C pile by shuffling together 2 randomly drawn tiles from the remainder of the A pack with the STASH tile (19) and place them facedown.
5. Make a D pile by shuffling together 2 randomly drawn tiles from the remainder of the A pack with the STASH tile (20) and place them facedown.
6. The three piles are placed on top of each other in the following order: B, then C with D placed underneath. The result is an E pile of 9 tiles, 2 of which are STASH tiles (19 & 20).
7. Return all remaining unused tiles to the box.
8. The EXIT tile (27) is placed in the center of the table and the Western warriors are placed upon it.

### Victory conditions

- The human player wins if they manage to exorcise the 2 POSSESSED. The game ends immediately once the second POSSESSED is exorcised.
- Any other result is a victory for the infernal player.

### Special rules

**The monster's lair**

Each time a STASH tile (19 or 20) is placed, the infernal player places a POSSESSED on it. This is the only way that the POSSESSED can be brought into play.

**Redemption by steel**

When a POSSESSED is killed, it is considered exorcised and regains consciousness.

1. The human player immediately takes control and selects a warrior board of their choice (CONDEMNED SCOUT or CONDEMNED BRUTE).
2. The corresponding miniature is placed in the same place as the exorcised POSSESSED. If the appearance of this warrior does not respect the tile occupation limit rule, it is moved to an adjacent tile.
3. The infernal player cancels a line of activation of their choice with a DAMAGE marker on that warrior's board.
4. Finally, the human player places an activation die on the warrior board with a value of their choice to determine its statistics until the beginning of the next preparation phase of the human player. This new warrior cannot be played as usual until the next preparation phase.

## Cut off the Head

*by Croc*

*Until now, all the efforts of the Redeemer seemed to be in vain. The Troglodytes were still infesting the tunnels with their legions, and the Westerners' losses were heavy. It was becoming clear that there was no point trying to fight on equal footing. Rather than continue to sacrifice prisoner after prisoner, the Humans gathered up all their information and established a new battle plan. Ridding the oozing tunnels of Troglodytes was no longer their aim; instead, they planned to remove that which they would miss the most, the Demon that holds their ranks together and gives them purpose. Without his guidance, the cowardly creatures would certainly give up the fight.*

*The Redeemer and his flock are therefore getting ready for a mission below, to seek out and destroy the infamous "Master of Souls".*

### Components to prepare

**Human player**

- Leader: The REDEEMER with the gifts PUNISH THE EVIL and LAYING ON OF HANDS.
- Fighters: a CONDEMNED SCOUT; a CONDEMNED BRUTE equipped with a SANCTIFIED WEAPON; a CONDEMNED BRUTE equipped with a STEEL SHIELD.
- Bonus: Two randomly drawn instinct cards.

**Infernal player**

- Demons: During the game, the MASTER OF SOULS can be brought into play (see special rules THE MONSTER'S LAIR).
- Hellhounds (x2)
- Troglodytes (x11)
- Prima matiera: Starting TP of 4.

DIRECTION token, TURN marker.

### Scenario setup

1. Remove the EXIT tile (27) and the PENTACLE tile (26) from the A pack of tiles and set them aside.
2. Make a pile B by shuffling the other tiles.
3. The EXIT tile (27) is placed in the center of the table and the Western warriors are placed upon it.

### Victory conditions

- The human player wins the game if they kill the MASTER OF SOULS.
- Any other result is a victory for the infernal player.

### Special rules

**Detection of evil**

In order to find their prey, the Western warriors are helped by a powerful relic that detects evil and shows them the way forward.

At the start of the game, the infernal player places the DIRECTION token next to one of the unexplored openings of the EXIT tile (27). They also place the TURN marker on the first space of the back of the PENTACLE tile (26).

When the corresponding opening is explored, the infernal player moves the TURN marker one space forward and places the DIRECTION token next to an unexplored opening of the newly explored tile.

If this tile does not have an unexplored opening, the infernal player places the DIRECTION token next to the unexplored opening of the closest tile.

When the TURN marker reaches 8, the next tile is not randomly drawn from the pile but the infernal player turns over the PENTACLE tile (26) and places it into the game.

**A wall of flesh and claws**

Whenever a tile is placed in play (with the exception of the PENTACLE tile (26)), the infernal player may place as many troglodytes as they wish on it by paying 1TP per troglodyte (still taking into account the tile occupation limit).

**The monster's lair**

When the PENTACLE tile (26) is placed into play, the infernal player places the MASTER OF SOULS on it. This is the only way the MASTER OF SOULS can be brought into play.

Troglodytes cannot be placed on the PENTACLE tile (26), the presence of the MASTER OF SOULS is too terrifying for these creatures.

**They come from everywhere**

Each time a STASH tile (19 to 22) is placed in play, the infernal player places two troglodytes on it.

## Cleanse by Fire

*by Croc*

*The years of exploration and suffering have borne fruit. Abominations have been discovered, horrors whose names are disputed by the demonologists and the theologians, but that the condemned and the Redeemers have already baptized: the spawners. They are the ones who give birth to the troglodyte herds; it is these vile creatures that have caused so many deaths and disappearances.*

*A Redeemer sinks into the darkness, carrying purifying fire. His warnings are unfortunately not enough: disasters succeed one another. If two of these monstrosities are set on fire, their exit will be cut off by a rock fall... It's time to fight...or die!*

### Components to prepare

**Human player**

- Leader: The REDEEMER with the gifts AURA OF COURAGE and ASSEMBLY.
- Fighters: a CONDEMNED SCOUT equipped with a BLUNDERBUSS; a CONDEMNED BRUTE equipped with a STEEL SHIELD; a CONDEMNED BRUTE.

**Infernal player**

- Demons: The BROOD MOTHER may be summoned. The TORTURER can also be brought into play (see special rules DEFENDING THE SPAWNERS).
- Hellhounds: The infernal player cannot use Hellhounds.
- Troglodytes (x11)
- Prima matiera: Starting TP of 6.

TRIGGERED EFFECT token (x3).

### Scenario setup

1. Remove the STASH tile (21), the EXIT tile (27), the TUNNEL tiles (46 to 48) and the PENTACLE tile (26) from the A pack of tiles and set them aside.
2. Make a B pile placed facedown by shuffling together the 8 following tiles: ALARM tile (3), HEALING FOUNTAIN tile (17), STASH tiles (19 and 20), CAVE tile (25), LAIR tiles (28 & 29), SANCTUARY tile (35).
3. Remove the last three tiles of the B pile and shuffle them together with the STASH tile (21). Replace these four tiles at the bottom of the B pile. The result is the C pile of 9 tiles with at least one STASH tile among the last four.
4. Shuffle together the EXIT tile (27) and the TUNNEL tile (46). Then place these under the C pile to make a D pile of 11 tiles.
5. The PENTACLE tile (26) is placed in the center of the table and the Western warriors are placed upon it.
6. Keep two TUNNEL tiles (47 & 48) close at hand.
7. Return all remaining unused tiles to the box.

### Victory conditions

- The human player wins if they manage to destroy the three spawners (see special THE SPAWNERS) and have one of their warriors is on the EXIT tile (27).
- Any other result is a victory for the infernal player.

### Special rules

**The spawners**

The STASH tiles (19 to 21) represent the spawners which produce the troglodytes. They must be destroyed by fire. Only the CONDEMNED BRUTES bearing flaming torches can burn the spawners. As soon as a CONDEMNED BRUTE enters a STASH tile (19 to 21), the spawner is destroyed. The CONDEMNED BRUTE is not required to finish their movement on the tile. A TRIGGERED EFFECT token is placed on the icon of the tile.

**Defending the spawners**

When the second STASH tile is placed, the infernal player places the TORTURER upon it. This is the only way that the TORTURER can be brought into play.

**Dead ends**

If the play area has no other unexplored openings and there are tiles left in the pile, replace the tile that has just been drawn at the bottom of the pile and use one of the TUNNEL tiles (47 & 48) that have been kept close at hand in its place.

## A Demon's Soul

*by Croc*

*Adramak, a name to shake the veterans of the subterranean depths. For years, this demon has sown death among the condemned and damned the Redeemers. Immortal, elusive, the abomination has made use of traps and incantations. But the opening of the heretical archives has revealed his only weak spot: Adramak has a soul. This impure soul is kept somewhere on the borders of the Malebolge, in an unexplored area of the Underworld. Equipped with an artifact that will allow them to identify the demonic odors, a Redeemer and his people sink into the darkness once again.*

### Components to prepare

**Human player**

- Leader: The REDEEMER with the gifts AURA OF COURAGE and ASSEMBLY, equipped with a BLUNDERBUSS.
- Fighters: a CONDEMNED SCOUT equipped with a NECKLACE OF EARS; a CONDEMNED SCOUT equipped with a BELT OF EXPLOSIVES; a CONDEMNED BRUTE equipped with a LAST CHANCE POTION.
- Bonus: a randomly drawn instinct card.

**Infernal player**

- Demons: During the game, the SORCERER can be brought into play (see special rules THE MONSTER'S LAIR).
- Hellhounds (x2)
- Troglodytes (x11)
- Prima matiera: Starting TP of 4.

DIRECTION token, TURN marker, ELUSIVE TALENT token (x1).

### Scenario setup

1. The STASH tiles (20 to 22) are not used for this game and are put back in the box.
2. Remove the STASH (19), EXIT (27) and PENTACLE tile (26) from the A pack of tiles and set them aside.
3. Make a B pile by shuffling the other tiles.
4. The EXIT tile (27) is placed in the center of the table and the Western warriors are placed upon it.

### Victory conditions

- The human player wins the game if they kill the SORCERER.
- Any other result is a victory for the infernal player.

### Special rules

**Detection of evil**

In order to find their prey, the Western warriors are helped by a powerful relic that detects evil and shows them the way forward.

At the start of the game, the infernal player places the DIRECTION token next to one of the unexplored openings of the EXIT tile (27). They also place the TURN marker on the first space of the back of the PENTACLE tile (26).

When the corresponding opening is explored, the infernal player moves the TURN marker one space forward and places the DIRECTION token next to an unexplored opening of the newly explored tile.

If this tile does not have an unexplored opening, the infernal player places the DIRECTION token next to the unexplored opening of the closest tile. When the TURN marker reaches space 8, the next tile is not drawn randomly but the infernal player turns over the PENTACLE tile (26) and places it into the game.

**A real labyrinth**

There must always be at least two unexplored openings on the play area. If placing a tile implies otherwise, discard it and draw another one.

**The monster's lair**

When the PENTACLE tile (26) is placed into play, the infernal player places the SORCERER on it. This is the only way that the SORCERER can be brought into play.

As long as the SORCERER's soul is not destroyed, he cannot be hit and has the elusive talent.

The infernal player places an ELUSIVE TALENT token on the SORCERER's board.

**The secret stash**

When the PENTACLE tile (26) is placed, the infernal player places a STASH tile (19) adjacent to a tile already in play. The SORCERER's soul is found there. At the end of their activation, a Western warrior can destroy the SORCERER's soul if they are on that tile and it does not contain any demon warriors. The EXPLOSIVE BELT can also destroy the SORCERER's soul if it is triggered on the tile where the soul is located. The SORCERER cannot enter the tile that contains his soul.

## The Ritual

*by Croc*

*The closing of the abyss and the defeat of the Master of Souls has given the Humans a short reprieve. The Redeemer is aware that they have little time, and has put together an ingenious plan – one approved by the Council of New Jerusalem: the purification of an entire section of the catacombs. Armed with his faith and protected by his warriors, the crusader hopes to banish all evil from the tunnels. In this way, he will truly wage war on the Troglodytes and their Demon masters rather than simply containing their continual attacks. But the one thing he didn't count on was the shrewdness of the Luciferians, who have heard about his plans. The underground hordes have gathered and are waiting impatiently, and hungrily, with Demons of Cruelty at their head, while the Redeemer makes his way down into their realm once again.*

### Components to prepare

**Human player**

- Fighters: a CONDEMNED SCOUT equipped with a BLUNDERBUSS; a CONDEMNED SCOUT; a CONDEMNED BRUTE equipped with a SHIELD OF STEEL; a CONDEMNED BRUTE.

**Infernal player**

- Demons: During the game, the TORTURER can be brought into play.
- Hellhounds: The infernal player cannot use Hellhounds.
- Troglodytes (x11)
- Prima matiera: Starting TP of 4.

TURN marker, ANGER token (x4), IMPRESSIVE TALENT token (x4), SEAL OF PROTECTION token (x3), REDEEMER's miniature.

### Scenario setup

1. Place the tiles as indicated by the following diagram (tiles 12, 45, 36 / 42, 26, 10 / 43, 39 / 46).
2. The REDEEMER is placed on the PENTACLE tile (26) but is only used to remind the infernal player of the objective of this scenario. He does not participate in this game. He is not considered as an active warrior.
3. The human player places the Western warriors on the tile or tiles of their choice.
4. Place a SEAL OF PROTECTION token at each opening indicated on the diagram (3 in total).
5. Return all remaining unused tiles to the box.
6. Remove the two TOUGH TROGLODYTE cards from the event card deck.

### Victory conditions

- The human player wins the game if the REDEEMER is still alive in the game when the ritual ends.
- The infernal player wins the game if they kill the REDEEMER.
- Any other result is a victory for the infernal player.

### Special rules

**Mapped battlefield**

No exploration is possible during this scenario.

The infernal player cannot use the SNEAKY CHARGE power of the board of destiny.

**Purifying evil**

The TURN marker is used to track the time the REDEEMER takes to carry out the ritual. At the start of the game, the human player places it on first space of the back of an unused tile. At the end of each human player's turn, not counting the first turn, the marker moves one space foward. When the TURN marker reaches 10, the REDEEMER has finished his ritual and the human player wins the game.

**Death to the servant of god**

As soon as a troglodyte enters the PENTACLE tile (26), the infernal player collects the SEAL OF PROTECTION token from the opening where the troglodyte entered. They then roll a combat die. Each SEAL OF PROTECTION token in their possession allows them to add a bonus of 2 to their roll value.

If the result is greater than or equal to 10, the REDEEMER is killed and the infernal player wins the game. Finally, the troglodyte is removed from the game. Demons cannot enter the PENTACLE tile (26).

**Spirit of sacrifice**

As soon as the infernal player gets their second SEAL OF PROTECTION token, all the Western warriors gain the impressive talent until the end of the game. The human player places an IMPRESSIVE TALENT token on each of their warrior boards.

As soon as the infernal player gets their third SEAL OF PROTECTION token, all the Western warriors gain +1 combat until the end of the game. The human player places an ANGER token on each of their warrior boards.

## The Root of all Evil

*by Philippe Masson*

*The opening of the heretical archives has raised many questions. Just how far is a man willing to go to risk his soul in the fight against evil? What vileness is acceptable, what horror is condemnable by the inquisitorial tribunal? These dilemmas are the responsibility of the theologians of New Jerusalem, because in the bowels of Hell, the fight dictates its own necessities.*

*Brother Marcellus has discovered that the influence of an old enemy is spreading again. This treacherous demon permeates the spirits of mortals and threatens to corrupt them. Consequently, he makes his way to the prohibited areas of the underground tunnels and prepares to invoke this merciless opponent with the help of forbidden texts. At the risk of damning his immortal soul, the Redeemer prepares the ritual to bring back the Master of Souls, and then slay him.*

### Components to prepare

**Human player**

- Leader: The REDEEMER with the gifts AURA OF ANGER and SACRIFICIAL STRIKE, equipped with a SACRED SYMBOL.
- Fighters: a CONDEMNED SCOUT equipped with a BLUNDERBUSS; a CONDEMNED SCOUT equipped with a NECKLACE OF EARS; a CONDEMNED BRUTE equipped with a STEEL SHIELD; a GUARDIAN.

**Infernal player**

- Demons: During the game, the MASTER OF SOULS can be brought into play (see special rules UNHOLY RITUAL and THE TABLES TURN).
- Hellhounds (x2)
- Troglodytes (x11)
- Prima matiera: Starting TP of 6.

SEAL OF PROTECTION token (x3).

### Scenario setup

1. The FOG tiles (4 & 5) and the TUNNEL tiles (9 to 11) are not used for this game and are put back in the box.
2. Remove the CARNIVOROUS TUNNEL tile (18), DEMONIC WELL tile (23), PENTACLE tile (26) and EXIT tile (27) from the A pack of tiles and set them aside.
3. Make a B pile (facedown) by shuffling together 5 randomly drawn tiles from the remainder of the A pack including the DEMONIC WELL tile (23).
4. Make a C pile (facedown) by shuffling together 5 randomly drawn tiles from the remainder of the A pack with the CARNIVOROUS TUNNEL tile (18).
5. Place the C pile on top of the B pile. Place the PENTACLE tile (26) on the bottom of this pile. Together these make a pile of 13 tiles with the PENTACLE tile (26) at the very bottom, in its final position.
6. The EXIT tile (27) is placed in the middle of the table and the Western warriors are placed upon it.
7. Return all remaining unused tiles to the box.

### Victory conditions

- The human player wins if they kill the MASTER OF SOULS.
- Any other result is a victory for the infernal player.

### Special rules

**Ritual wound (action)**

Self-inflicting a ritual wound is an action.

When a Western warrior is on the CARNIVOROUS TUNNEL tile (18) or the DEMONIC WELL tile (23) they can inflict a ritual wound upon themselves. When the REDEEMER is on the PENTACLE tile (26) he can also inflict a ritual wound upon himself.

A Western warrior cannot inflict a ritual wound upon themselves on a tile containing a SEAL OF PROTECTION token.

If a Western warrior self-inflicts a ritual wound the human player places a SEAL OF PROTECTION token on the tile, then places two DAMAGE markers in the space of the line of activation of their choice on the warrior.

The resistant talent cannot be used during a ritual wound action.

**Unholy ritual**

To invoke the MASTER OF SOULS the Western warriors must complete three rituals. The moment when the CARNIVOROUS TUNNEL tile (18), DEMONIC WELL tile (23) and PENTACLE tile (26) each contain a SEAL OF PROTECTION token – the MASTER OF SOULS is brought into play on the PENTACLE tile (26).

If the MASTER OF SOULS cannot enter into play on the PENTACLE tile (26) due to its occupation limit, the infernal player chooses and sacrifices one of their warriors present on that tile to make way for the demon.

This is one of the only ways that the MASTER OF SOULS can be brought into play (see special rule THE TABLES TURN).

**The tables turn**

If the REDEEMER is killed the MASTER OF SOULS immediately enters into play on the tile where the REDEEMER was.

If the MASTER OF SOULS cannot be brought into play due to the occupation limit of the tile then the infernal player chooses and sacrifices one of their warriors present on the tile to make way for the demon.

This is the only other way that the MASTER OF SOULS can be brought into play (see special rule UNHOLY RITUAL).

**Occupants of hell**

Infernal warriors are immune to the effects of the CARNIVOROUS TUNNEL tile (18).

## Plunder

Among these bands of thieves, the Schwarze Brüderlichkeit band stands out for their ability to defend themselves. Former mercenaries, they have turned to desecrating tombs. A condemned has spotted the chalk marks drawn by these brigands while the Redeemer of his unit orders the repatriation of their remains. These symbols indicate that a tomb is in the vicinity, certainly filled with precious goods. He later escapes the surveillance of his Redeemer and, accompanied by bandits of his acquaintance, sets off in search of the loot.*

*But everything in Hell is there for good reason. And all of it is being watched.*

**Human player**

- Fighters: a CONDEMNED SCOUT equipped with a BLUNDERBUSS; a CONDEMNED SCOUT; a CONDEMNED BRUTE equipped with a STEEL SHIELD; a GUARDIAN; a SWORDSWOMAN.

**Infernal player**

- Demons: During the game, the HAUNTER can be brought into play (see special rules THE MONSTER'S LAIR).
- Hellhounds (x2)
- Troglodytes (x11)
- Prima matiera: Starting TP of 6.

CHEST token (x3).

**Purification**

At the start of the game, the HEALING FOUNTAIN tile (16) is the only tile to have been purified.

At the start of the preparation phase of the human player, a Western warrior can purify a desecrated tile on which they are placed if they have a MIRACULOUS WATER token. To do this, they spend all their movement (minimum spend 1) and place the MIRACULOUS WATER token in the MIRACULOUS WATER reserve. They then place the desecrated tile under the newly purified tile. Any effects generated by the purified tile's tile power come into immediate effect.

Once purified, the STASH tiles (19, 20 & 22) and the TOMB tile (30) can no longer be desecrated.

**Desecration (action)**

At the start of the threat phase, the BLOATED can desecrate a purified tile on which they are located. To do this, they must spend 4 TP and give up their action for this round. They then place the purified tile under the newly desecrated tile. Any effects generated by the desecrated tile's tile power come into immediate effect.

If the BLOATED desecrates a HEALING FOUNTAIN tile (16 or 17) that contains MIRACULOUS WATER tokens, the tokens are returned to the MIRACULOUS WATER reserve.

**The relics**

When a Western warrior enters a STASH tile (19, 20 & 22) or the TOMB tile (30) for the first time, they take the first equipment card from the top of the relic deck.

**Sacred symbol**

The tile in which any warrior equipped with the SACRED SYMBOL equipment card is located **cannot** be desecrated.
