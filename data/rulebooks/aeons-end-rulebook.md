# Aeon's End Rulebook

## Table of Contents

*Aeon's End is a cooperative deck-building game for 1 to 4 players in which your deck is never shuffled. Playing as breach mages defending the last bastion of humanity, Gravehold, you cast spells through mystical breaches to defeat an invading nemesis. The signature twist is a variable turn order — players and the nemesis act in a random sequence each round — and a deck you carefully sculpt because you always know what you'll draw next. Defeat the nemesis before Gravehold is overrun or the mages are exhausted.*

## Object of the Game

Your goal is to defeat the nemesis before your home, Gravehold, is overrun or the players are exhausted. Each round, the players and the nemesis take turns in a random order determined by the turn order deck. On a player's turn, they cast spells, acquire gems/relics/spells from the supply, manipulate their breaches, and use their unique ability.

## Key Components

- **Player cards** come in three types: **Gems** (played to give **aether**, the main currency — spent to gain cards, gain charges, and focus breaches), **Spells** (the main way to deal damage — must be prepped to a breach before being cast), and **Relics** (instantaneous effects). Cards are gained from the supply by spending aether equal to their cost; gained cards go to your discard pile.
- **Breaches** are the double-sided conduits through which spells are cast. Each has an **Open Cost** (aether to flip it to its opened side, permanently) and a **Focus Cost** (aether to rotate it 90° clockwise toward opening). A spell may only be prepped to an **opened** breach, or to a **closed** breach on the turn it was focused. Some breaches have effects (e.g., +1 damage on cast) while opened. Breaches are adjacent to the one or two physically next to them.
- **Nemesis cards** are the nemesis's **Attacks** (resolved immediately, then discarded), **Minions** (enter play with life, staying until defeated, with persistent effects), and **Powers** (build up over turns until they resolve). Basic cards work with any nemesis; unique cards belong to a specific one.
- **Turn order deck:** 9 cards used to determine the order of play.
- **Life dials** track the life of Gravehold and the nemesis; **life tokens** track players' and minions' life; **charge tokens** fuel abilities.

Each breach mage has a player mat with a **breach setup**, starting hand, starting deck, and a unique **ability** activated when charge tokens fill all its spaces (then all are removed).

## Turn Order

At the start of the game and after each player or nemesis turn ends, draw a card from the turn order deck to determine who acts next, placing it on the discard pile. When the deck is empty, shuffle the discards to replenish it. (A standard deck contains player turn order cards and nemesis turn order cards.)

## Player Turn

A player's turn has three phases in order:

### 1. Casting Phase

You **may** cast any prepped spells in **opened** breaches; you **must** cast any prepped spells in **closed** breaches (a spell prepped to a focused breach on the previous turn). Choose the order. When a spell is cast, discard it to the top of your discard pile, then resolve the effect after "Cast:". A spell deals damage to a single minion or the nemesis unless specified. Damage to a minion removes life tokens (discarded at 0); damage to the nemesis reduces its dial (players win at 0). A breach's "+1 damage on cast" applies to any spell cast from it.

### 2. Main Phase

Resolve the following actions in any order, any number of times:

1. **Play a gem or relic card** (execute all its text; gain aether even if you don't spend it).
2. **Gain a card** from the supply by spending aether equal to its cost (placed in your discard pile).
3. **Gain a charge** by paying 2 aether (place a charge token on your mat; you cannot pay for allies, nor exceed your ability's required charges).
4. **Focus a breach** by paying its focus cost (rotate it 90° clockwise; the fourth focus opens it).
5. **Open a breach** by paying its open cost (flip it to opened; it stays opened all game).
6. **Prep a spell to a breach** (place a spell from hand onto an opened breach, or a breach focused this turn).
7. **Resolve a "While prepped" effect.**
8. **Resolve a "TO DISCARD:" effect.**

### 3. Draw Phase

Place all gems and relics you played this turn on top of your discard pile in any order, then draw from the top of your deck until you have five cards in hand. (Because the deck is never shuffled, you control your future draws by choosing your discard order.)

You may sometimes cast another player's prepped spell; you become the "you" on that card and make all its decisions, placing it on that ally's discard pile.

## Nemesis Turn

1. **Main phase:** from oldest to newest, resolve the effects of each minion and power card the nemesis has in play (persistent minion effects, power counters advancing/resolving, etc.).
2. **Draw phase:** draw a card from the nemesis deck. Resolve an **attack** immediately (then discard it); place a **minion** or **power** into play with the appropriate life/power tokens, resolving any "IMMEDIATELY:" effect. Each nemesis has a unique **Unleash** effect triggered by various cards.

## Exhausted Players

If a player's life is reduced to zero, that player is **exhausted**. Resolve, in order: the nemesis's Unleash effect **twice**; the exhausted player destroys one of their breaches (discarding any prepped spell — destroyed breaches are gone for good); and the player discards all their charge tokens. Exhausted players keep participating but cannot gain life, and effects targeting "the lowest life" player target the lowest **non-exhausted** player.

## Game End

When an end-game condition is met, play ends immediately. Players **win** if the nemesis has no cards in its deck and no minions or powers in play, **or** the nemesis has 0 life. Players **lose** if all players are exhausted, **or** Gravehold has 0 life (or a nemesis's specific loss condition is met).

## Variable Difficulty

Play the nemesis mat as written for normal mode. **Beginner:** players start with 12 life, Gravehold 35, nemesis −10 life. **Expert:** use the nemesis's Increased Difficulty rules. **Extinction:** use Increased Difficulty plus players start with 8 life, Gravehold 25, nemesis +10 life.

## Solo Play

Play as multiple mages you control (following the rules for that player count), or a true solo experience using a turn order deck of 3 player and 2 nemesis cards (you take a turn on any player card drawn), where you are your own ally.
