# Myrmes: Upgraded Colony Rulebook

## Table of Contents

*Upgraded colony (Fourmilière améliorée) is an official Ystari mini-expansion for Myrmes. It adds 4 alternative colony tiles which replace the default powers of the ant colony levels.*

## Upgraded Colony

When advancing a new colony level, a player can take one of the 4 alternative colony tiles (of the level he would have reached normally) and place it on his personal board, above the default level. He has now the new powers described below until the end of the game (instead of normal ones). No extra cost is needed (only the basic price of advancing a colony level).

*Note: when using the level +1 bonus, you take into consideration the powers actually in place.*

## 1) Level I

**First Level I tile**

- **Level I (grey):** identic to the level III of the normal game (worker action: 1 food for 2 victory points)
- **Level I (white):** for this player only, mushrooms are food when placing pheromone tile

**Second Level I tile**

- **Level I (grey):** for a worker action, this player can place or replace one tunnel according to standard rules, adjacent to his colours (without getting earth)
- **Level I (white):** each time a worker is going out, the player has one virtual warrior only to step on one opponent pheromone.

## 2) Level II

- **Level II (grey):** for a worker action, this player doesn't discard any cube this turn during phase 6
- **Level II (white):** each time a worker is going out, the player has one virtual warrior only to hunt prey

*(French text adds: not during winter and not for opposing pheromones.)*

## 3) Level III

- **Level III (grey):** for a worker action, the player gets 2 larvae
- **Level III (white):** the player can now place 2 nurses on the same space during the phase 5
