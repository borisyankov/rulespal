# Yu-Gi-Oh! Trading Card Game Rulebook

## Table of Contents

*The Yu-Gi-Oh! TRADING CARD GAME casts two Duelists in a battle of monsters, magic, and traps. Summon fearsome monsters, back them with Spell and Trap Cards, and attack to drain your opponent's Life Points from 8000 to zero. Fast, combo-driven, and famous for dramatic reversals, Yu-Gi-Oh! rewards clever sequencing and reading your opponent. This rulebook covers the fundamental rules of a standard two-player Duel.*

## Object of the Game

Each player starts with **8000 Life Points**. You win a Duel by any of these:
- Reducing your opponent's **Life Points to 0**.
- Your opponent **cannot draw** a card when required (their Deck is empty).
- A specific card's effect declares you the winner (e.g., Exodia).

A Match is best-of-three Duels.

## The Cards

Your **Main Deck** is **40 to 60 cards**, with no more than **3 copies** of any card (some cards are Limited to 1 or Semi-Limited to 2 by the official Forbidden & Limited list). A separate **Extra Deck** (up to 15 cards) holds Fusion, Synchro, Xyz, and Link Monsters. An optional **Side Deck** (up to 15) is for swapping between Duels.

### Monster Cards

Monsters are the core of your offense and defense:

- **Normal Monsters** (yellow) have no effect — just stats and flavor.
- **Effect Monsters** (orange) have special abilities.
- **Ritual, Fusion, Synchro, Xyz, Pendulum, Link** monsters are special summons requiring specific conditions.

Key monster stats:
- **Level / Rank** (stars): determines how it can be Summoned.
- **ATK** (Attack): damage it deals in battle.
- **DEF** (Defense): its strength while in Defense Position.
- **Attribute** (DARK, LIGHT, EARTH, WATER, FIRE, WIND, DIVINE) and **Type** (Dragon, Spellcaster, Warrior, etc.).

### Spell and Trap Cards

- **Spell Cards** (green): played from your hand during your Main Phase (Quick-Play Spells can be used more freely). Types include Normal, Continuous, Equip, Field, Quick-Play, and Ritual.
- **Trap Cards** (purple): must first be **Set** face-down, and can only be activated on a **later turn** (Counter and Normal Traps). They spring surprises on your opponent.

## The Field

Each player's side has: a **Main Monster Zone** (5 spaces), a **Spell & Trap Zone** (5 spaces), a **Field Zone**, the **Graveyard**, the **Deck**, the **Extra Deck**, and (in modern layouts) two shared **Extra Monster Zones** for Extra Deck monsters.

## Starting the Game

1. Shuffle your Deck; decide who goes first (rock-paper-scissors or a die roll).
2. Both players draw **5 cards**.
3. The player going **first does not draw** on their first turn.

## Turn Structure (Phases)

A turn has six phases, in order:

### 1. Draw Phase
Draw 1 card from the top of your Deck (the first player skips this on turn one). If you can't draw, you lose.

### 2. Standby Phase
Resolve any effects that occur here.

### 3. Main Phase 1
The heart of your turn. You may:
- **Normal Summon or Set** one monster (once per turn). Monsters Level 4 or lower are Summoned directly; Level 5–6 require **Tributing 1** monster, Level 7+ require **Tributing 2**.
- **Special Summon** any number of monsters whose conditions are met (Fusion, Synchro, Xyz, Link, effects, etc.).
- Change the **battle position** of monsters (each once per turn, not the turn they were Summoned).
- **Activate or Set** Spell and Trap Cards.
- Activate monster effects.

### 4. Battle Phase
Declare attacks with your monsters (you cannot attack on the very first turn of the Duel — the player going first skips their first Battle Phase). Steps:
- **Start Step / Battle Step:** choose an attacking monster and a target.
- **Damage Step:** compare stats and apply battle results (see below).
- You may attack with **each monster once** per Battle Phase.

### 5. Main Phase 2
A second Main Phase to Set traps, summon, or activate cards you held back (you may not Normal Summon again if you already did in Main Phase 1).

### 6. End Phase
End-of-turn effects resolve. If you hold more than **6 cards**, discard down to 6. Then pass to your opponent.

## Battle Position

A monster is in one of two positions:

- **Attack Position** (upright, face-up): uses its **ATK**; can attack.
- **Defense Position** (sideways): uses its **DEF**; cannot attack. A monster may be **Set** face-down in Defense Position, hidden until flipped.

## Battle and Damage

When your monster attacks:

- **Attacking a monster in Attack Position:** compare ATK values. The lower-ATK monster is **destroyed**; its controller takes damage equal to the **difference** in ATK. If both are equal, both are destroyed and no one takes damage.
- **Attacking a monster in Defense Position:** compare the attacker's **ATK** to the defender's **DEF**. If ATK is higher, the defending monster is destroyed (no Life Point damage unless the attacker has piercing). If ATK is lower, **no monster is destroyed** but the attacker's controller takes the difference as damage. If equal, nothing happens.
- **Direct attack:** if your opponent has **no monsters**, your attacking monster deals its full **ATK** to their Life Points directly.

Destroyed monsters go to the **Graveyard**.

## Special Summoning (Extra Deck)

- **Fusion:** combine specific monsters (usually via Polymerization) into a Fusion Monster from the Extra Deck.
- **Synchro:** send a Tuner monster plus non-Tuner monsters whose Levels total the Synchro Monster's Level.
- **Xyz:** overlay two or more monsters of the same Level to Summon an Xyz Monster of that Rank, using the materials for its effects.
- **Link:** send monsters equal to the Link Monster's arrows; Link Monsters have no DEF and point to zones that enable further Extra Deck summons.

These summons don't use your once-per-turn Normal Summon.

## Chains and Spell Speed

When a card or effect is activated, the opponent may respond, forming a **Chain**. The Chain resolves in **reverse order** (last activated resolves first). **Spell Speed** governs what can respond: Normal Spells and most monster effects are Spell Speed 1; Quick-Play Spells and most Traps are Spell Speed 2; Counter Traps are Spell Speed 3 and can only be answered by other Counter Traps.

## Winning

The Duel ends the moment a player's **Life Points reach 0**, they **can't draw** when required, or a card effect grants victory. Win two Duels to take the Match. Between Duels, players may adjust their deck using the **Side Deck**.

## Deckbuilding Notes

A consistent Deck balances monsters that can be Summoned early, Spells that develop your board and search key pieces, and Traps that disrupt the opponent. Modern Yu-Gi-Oh! emphasizes **combos** that chain Special Summons from the Extra Deck; respect the Forbidden & Limited list when building.
