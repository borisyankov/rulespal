# Mall Madness Rulebook

## Table of Contents

*Ready, set, shop! Move your pawn around the shopping mall, using your ATM card to purchase items on your shopping list. The game unit will tell you which stores have sales and clearances, and where to move on your turn. The first player to purchase six items and make it back to their parking lot wins!*

## Contents

GAMEBOARD, ELECTRONIC GAME UNIT, 4 PLASTIC MALL WALLS, 7 PAWN STANDS, 30 PLASTIC CLIPS, MALL MADNESS MONEY, 4 SHOPPERS, 4 CASH CARDS, 4 SHOPPING LISTS, 2 SALE SIGNS, CLEARANCE SIGN, 8 FOOD TOKENS, 4 FLOOR PATHWAYS, 22 MALL LOCATIONS, AND 2 ELEVATORS

## Quickstart

1. Build the mall and set up the game as directed on page 2.
2. Press and hold the New/Celebration button for two seconds to start the game.
3. The game unit will announce one color at a time. When you hear your color announced, press the ENTER button.
4. Place the Sale and Clearance signs at the corresponding stores as directed by the game unit. Press the ENTER button when all three signs have been placed.
5. Listen for the game unit to announce whose turn it is, then move your shopper as directed.

Press Enter to end your turn and begin the next player's turn.

That's all you need to know to get started—see the rest of the rules for complete instructions!

## The First Time You Play

* Punch the cardboard parts out of their sheets.
* Insert batteries into the game unit. (See diagram on back page.)

## Building the Mall

1. Unfold the gameboard and place it in the center of the play area.
2. Place the game unit on the gameboard where indicated. Insert the round feet into the holes.
3. Place the 4 mall walls on the gameboard. Insert the tab on the bottom of each wall into a hole in the gameboard as shown.
4. Place the upper floor pathway onto the mall as shown, making sure to place the colored squares facedown over their matching color start space.
5. Insert the 22 cardboard locations and 2 elevators into the mall walls as shown. Insert the elevators on opposite ends of the mall where indicated.
6. Insert the shoppers, sale, and clearance signs into the black pawn stands.

## Set Up the Game

1. Choose which shopper you'd like to be and place the pawn on its start space. Then take the corresponding shopping list and cash card and place them in front of you.
2. Each player takes 6 plastic clips. You'll use these to check off the items you buy throughout the game.
3. Place the food tokens in a pile near the gameboard.
4. Choose a player to be the banker. The banker gives each player:
   * 1x $50 bill
   * 3x $20 bills
   * 3x $10 bills
   * 2x $5 bills

   Total = $150

The banker is in charge of collecting and distributing each shopper's money throughout the game.

## Let's Shop!

1. To start a new game press and hold the New/Celebration button for 2 seconds.
2. Each player logs in using the game unit's instructions. When you hear your color announced, press the ENTER button. If you don't log in fast enough, don't worry—you'll get one more chance before the game begins.
3. After all players have logged in, the unit will announce the store locations for one clearance and two sales. Place the clearance and sale signs at the corresponding stores. Press the ENTER button when all three signs have been placed.
4. Listen for the unit to announce whose turn it is.

*Note: During the game, new sales and clearances will be announced. When this happens, move the signs to the new locations.*

## On Your Turn

The game unit will tell you to move in one of two ways:

1. **Move to a specific location.** Move your shopper as instructed and follow any other instructions given. The unit may direct you to move another player's shopper as well as your own. (See Specific Game Unit Instructions and What They Mean for details.)
2. **Move a number of spaces.** You may move up to that number of spaces. If you have any food tokens, you may use one of them to add up to three spaces to your move. (See Moving by Number of Spaces for more info.)

*If you missed something that was said, press the "?" button to repeat the last announcement.*

Try to move to ONE of the following:

* A store where you'll be able buy something on your list. But check how much the items on your list cost first to make sure you have enough cash. Smart shoppers will head to a store that's having a sale or clearance. See Buying an Item.
* An ATM if you need cash. After you've bought a few items, you may need a trip here! See Getting Cash.
* A food location (Funny Lickin' Ice Cream Shop, the Movie Theatre, or the Food Court) to pick up a food token. Food tokens give you energy—they let you add up to three spaces to a future move.

Can't reach any of these places? Get as close as you can.

After you've made your move, press ENTER to end your turn. The unit will announce the next player's turn.

## Moving by Number of Spaces

On your first turn, the game unit will direct you to move a number of spaces.

* You can move in any direction, EXCEPT diagonally.
* You can move through and share spaces with other shoppers.
* You can enter a store only through its door. You can't move through store walls.
* Half of the mall's stores and the ATM are located upstairs, on the second floor. You can move from the first floor to the second and back by using the elevators at either end of the mall. Moving from one floor to the other counts as one space.

## Using Food Tokens

Food gives you fuel! If the game unit directs you to move a number of spaces, you can use a food token to add up to three spaces to your move. For example, when you hear: "Red, move five," you can use a food token to increase your move up to eight.

After you've used a food token, place it back in the pile.

*NOTE: You can never have more than two food tokens in front of you at any time.*

## Specific Game Unit Instructions and What They Mean

**"Go to the Rest Room."**
Move your shopper to the Rest Rooms space and press the ENTER button to end your turn.

**"Meet a friend at the Movies."**
Move your shopper and any other shopper to the Movie Theatre and take one food token each (see Using Food Tokens). Press the ENTER button to end your turn.

**"Move (number of spaces) and send a friend anywhere."**
Move your shopper as directed, then move any other shopper to any space on the board. Press the ENTER button to end your turn.

**"Move (number of spaces) and send a friend to Noah's Arcade."**
Move your shopper as directed, then move any other shopper to the Arcade. Press the ENTER button to end your turn.

**"Move (number of spaces) and send a friend to Funny Lickin'."**
Move your shopper as directed, then move any other shopper to Funny Lickin' Ice Cream Shop. That shopper can take a food token (see Food Tokens). Press the ENTER button to end your turn.

**"You set off the security alarm! Stay put while they remove the tag."**
Don't move. Press the ENTER button to end your turn.

**"Meet all shoppers at the ATM!"**
You and all other shoppers must move your pawns to the ATM space. All shoppers, starting with you, will be prompted to take turns inserting their card into the ATM slot (see Getting Cash). Once all players have taken their cash, press the ENTER button to end your turn.

## Buying an Item

If you've moved to a store and want to buy something, put your cash card into the BUYING slot. You'll hear one of the following phrases:

**"Cha-ching!"**
This classic cash register sound means you can make the purchase. Pay the banker the amount listed on your shopping list with cash.

* Pay REGULAR price if there are no Sale or Clearance signs in the store.
* Pay SALE price if there is a Sale sign in the store.
* Pay CLEARANCE price if there is a Clearance sign in the store.

Move one of your clips to the blank box next to the item you just purchased on your shopping list. Then press ENTER to end your turn.

**"Hey, this is on clearance!"**
Regardless of any signs in the store, pay the banker the CLEARANCE price for this item. Move one of your clips to the blank box next to the item on your shopping list. Then press the ENTER button to end your turn.

**"Sorry we're out of stock. Try again later" or "Woah, long line. Try again later."**
You can't buy anything right now. Press the ENTER button to end your turn. You can choose to stay in this store on your next turn and try to buy the item again.

If you moved to a store but don't have enough cash to buy an item, press the ENTER button to end your turn.

If you try to buy an item and then discover that you didn't have enough cash (or realized that you already bought the item), you must cancel the purchase. Re-insert your cash card into the BUYING slot. When your purchase has been canceled, you'll hear: "Try again later." Press the ENTER button to end your turn.

## Getting Cash

After buying a few items, you may need to go to the ATM to get more cash. Here's what to do:

* Move to the ATM space on the second floor and insert your cash card into the ATM slot.
* The banker gives you the amount of money the game unit announces.

You can stay at the ATM for as many turns as you'd like, but after the second consecutive turn you'll receive only $20 per turn. You'll only be able to receive the full amount announced by the unit after you leave and come back.

If you accidentally put your cash card into the ATM slot, simply cancel the withdrawal by reinserting your card into the ATM slot. When the transaction has been canceled, you'll hear "Try again later." Insert your ATM card into the buying slot to make a purchase, or Press the ENTER button to end your turn.

## Winning the Game

Once a player has purchased six items, the game voice will announce "Now get to the parking lot to win!"

If you reach your color parking lot before anyone else buys their six items and reaches their parking lot, you win!

Press the NEW/CELEBRATION button to hear your winning music.

## Storage

Before storing the game, carefully remove the upper floor pathway and take the mall apart. Then return all of the pieces to the box and close the lid for easy storage.

*Note: You do not need to remove the storefronts from the plastic walls.*
