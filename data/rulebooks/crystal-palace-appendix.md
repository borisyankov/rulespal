# Crystal Palace Appendix Rulebook

## Table of Contents

*This Appendix accompanies the main Crystal Palace rulebook. It provides the full icon overview referenced throughout the rules, additional details on several game elements, worked examples for the Patent and Character cards that need extra explanation, and two sample nation objectives.*

*Translated from the official German-language Appendix ("Anhang"), published by Feuerland Spiele, as no English-language edition of this document could be located; all other Crystal Palace materials in this app are transcribed directly from the official English rulebook.*

## Symbol Overview

- **Lightning bolt banner (gray):** Receive the indicated bonus immediately and only once.
- **Orange banner "4":** Receive / use the effect in Phase 4.
- **Blue banner "5":** Receive / use the effect in Phase 5.
- **Green banner "6":** Receive / use the effect in Phase 6.
- **Gold medal:** Receive the indicated victory points immediately and only once.
- **Silver medal:** Receive the indicated victory points at game end.
- **Green banner "6" on a Publisher tile:** The number of your effects that you receive in Phase 6.3 (at the Black Market, on Research tiles, on the Pocket Watch).
- **Pound icon:** Pound.
- **Gear icon:** Gear.
- **Lightbulb icon:** Energy.
- **Booklet icon:** Newspaper.
- **Megaphone icon:** Buzz (steps on the Buzz track).
- **Die icon:** Die.
- **Coin/pound octagon icon:** Income (steps on the Income track).
- **Framed icon:** Share.
- **Blue card back:** Patent card.
- **Card with your player-color banner (blue/brown):** Your own Prototype card.
- **Card with your player-color banner (red/brown):** Your own Character card.
- **Card with mixed banner:** Your own Prototype or Character card.
- **Two overlapping cards, no link icon:** A set of one Prototype card and one Character card (no link required).
- **Two overlapping cards with a star:** A link between a Character card and a Prototype card.
- **Person silhouette:** You may perform an Assistant action.
- **Person silhouette with red cross:** You may perform an Assistant action. At the Black Market, this action is free.
- **"Black Market" banner with downward red arrow:** Place one of your Assistants in the Black Market on the next lower free space. If there is no free space below it, it leaves the Black Market.
- **"Black Market" banner with person silhouette:** Your own Assistants in the Black Market.
- **Player marker on "II":** Player marker on Position II in Westminster.
- **Player marker on "III":** Player marker on Position III in Westminster.
- **Player marker on "IV":** Player marker on Position IV in Westminster.
- **Player marker on "V":** Player marker on Position V in Westminster.
- **Player marker with forward arrow:** Move your player marker in Westminster one space forward. You receive the immediate bonus.
- **Player marker with lightning bolt:** Receive the immediate bonus of your current position in Westminster once again.
- **Pocket Watch icon:** Immediately take the Pocket Watch and decide on a side. Details on the Pocket Watch can be found below.
- **"LOAN" tile (open):** Open loan.
- **"REPAID -5" tile:** Repaid loan.
- **"REPAID/LOAN" crossed out:** Loan of any kind (repaid or not).
- **Green-backed Research tile:** Research tile with a green backside (British Museum).
- **Half green / half violet Research tile:** Research tile of any kind (green or violet backside).
- **Ticket icon:** Level reached for the objective on your player mat.
- **x = y:** For each element on the left side you receive 1x the element on the right side. You only need to have the element, not spend it.
- **x : y:** If you meet the condition on the left side, you receive the reward on the right side.
- **Arrow (→):** In exchange for giving up the element on the left side, you receive the element on the right side. You may make this exchange as many times as you like.
- **Arrow with "1x":** In exchange for giving up the element on the left side, you receive the element on the right side. You may make this exchange exactly once.

## Details on Various Game Elements

### The Pocket Watch

You can only obtain the Pocket Watch through cards. When you receive the Watch, you immediately choose one of its two sides and place the Watch with that side face up next to your player mat. One side gives you 2 victory points immediately and only once, while the other side (for as long as you have the Watch) gives you 3 pounds in Phase 6.3. The Watch remains on this side in front of you until another player receives the Watch. If another player receives the Watch, you must immediately hand it over to them. If you already have the Watch and would receive it again, you may choose a side again. If you choose the victory point side, you receive the 2 victory points again, if applicable.

### Examples for Violet Research Tiles

- Receive 1 Research tile with a green backside every round in Phase 6.3. You can choose between the tiles at the British Museum (2) or a random face-down one. If you thereby receive one with an effect in Phase 6.3, you may use it directly.
- Receive 1 Patent card immediately and only once. You can choose between the Patent cards at the Patent Office (1) and the top card of the Patent card deck.

### Examples for Green Research Tiles

- Receive 1 pound every round in Phase 6.3 for each of your Character cards.
- Receive 2 income and 1 Patent card immediately and only once. You can choose between the Patent cards at the Patent Office (1) and the top card of the Patent card deck.

## Publisher Tiles

If you meet several conditions, the rewards are not added together — only the highest reward whose condition you meet counts. The conditions are minimum values, so you also meet them if you have even more than the required condition. If your value lies between two tiers, you receive the reward for the lower tier.

*Example: With an income of 7 you would receive 3 Buzz as a reward. With an income of 10 you would receive 4 Buzz as a reward.*

## Cards (General)

If a card shows the backside of a Research tile as its effect, you receive an unspecified Research tile with a green backside. You can choose between the Research tiles at the British Museum (2) or a random face-down one.

If a card shows the front side of a specific Research tile as its effect, you receive exactly that Research tile with a violet backside. The backside also indicates the color and number of the card through which you receive the tile.

## Examples for Objectives on the Player Mats

**Spain**

- Level 1: Have at least 1 Research tile with an immediate effect.
- Level 2: Have at least 2 Research tiles with an immediate effect.
- Level 3: Have at least 3 Research tiles with an immediate effect.

**Italy**

- Level 1: Your player marker has reached or exceeded space 5 on the Buzz track.
- Level 2: Your player marker has reached or exceeded space 15 on the Buzz track.
- Level 3: Your player marker has reached or exceeded space 25 on the Buzz track.
