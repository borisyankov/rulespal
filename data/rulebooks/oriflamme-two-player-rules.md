# Oriflamme — 2 Players Rules

## Table of Contents

*The official Studio H "Oriflamme — 2 Players Rules" supplement. These rules replace the corresponding sections of the base rulebook; everything not mentioned here is unchanged.*

## Setup

* Each player takes 2 families of 10 cards and sets aside 3 random cards from each family face down. (Example: a player uses the red and green families, while his opponent plays with the blue and yellow).
* Each player randomly draws 3 cards from each of his families to make their starting hand. (Example: 3 red and 3 green cards).
* Each player shuffles his 8 remaining cards into a face down draw pile, alternating between cards from each family. (Example: sequencing 1 red and 1 green card).
* The oldest player takes the First Player tile and places the Resolution Direction tile.
* Each player takes 1 Influence point in front of them. All the remaining tokens, are placed within reach, forming the supply.

## Game Round

### 1/ Placement Phase

* Starting with the first player, each player secretly picks a card from their hand and places it face down in the Queue. Each player places 2 cards one at a time, following this sequence: first player, second player, first player, second player.

**Important:** You can place two cards from the same family or from different families. You cannot stack together cards from different families.

### 2/ Resolution Phase

The rules are unchanged.

Each player gains Influence points from the cards in both their families.

However, both families belonging to the same player are still considered opposed, except for the Ambush.

Examples: A player uses the red and green families. The red Lord does not generate 1 extra Influence point if it is adjacent to a green card.

If the red Soldier eliminates the green Ambush, the player does not gain 4 Influence points and the Soldier is not discarded.

### 3/ Draw Phase

At the end of the resolution phase, each player draws 2 addtionnal cards from their draw pile.

The First player token passes to the second player and a new round begins.

## End of Game

After 6 rounds of play, whoever accumulated the most Influence points wins the game! Both players will have 2 cards left in hand, which will not be played.
