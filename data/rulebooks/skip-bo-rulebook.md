# Skip-Bo Rulebook

## Table of Contents

*Skip-Bo is the classic sequencing card game from the makers of UNO. Race to empty your personal Stock pile by building shared Building piles up in numerical order from 1 to 12, using skill, strategy, and wild Skip-Bo cards. For 2 to 6 players, individually or in partnerships. Ages 7 and up.*

## Object

Be the first player to play every card from your **Stock pile** by adding cards, in numerical order 1 through 12, onto the shared Building piles.

## Components

A deck of **162 cards**: **144 number cards** (values 1 through 12) plus **18 Skip-Bo cards**, which are **wild** and may stand for any number. Remove any blank cards—they are not used.

## The Piles

- **Stock pile** — each player has one, placed facedown to their right, with its top card turned faceup. Emptying this pile wins the game.
- **Draw pile** — the remaining cards after the deal, facedown in the center.
- **Building piles** — up to **four** shared piles built in the center during play. Each is built **upward in sequence, 1 to 12**. Only a **1** (or a wild Skip-Bo card) may start one. When a pile reaches 12 it is complete, removed, and its space can be reused.
- **Discard piles** — each player may build up to **four** discard piles to the left of their Stock pile. Cards may be stacked in any order, but only the **top card** of each is available to play.

## Setup

1. Shuffle the deck. Each player draws a card; the highest deals (Skip-Bo cards don't count for this). The deal passes to the left after each game.
2. The dealer deals each player a facedown **Stock pile**:
   - **2–4 players:** 30 cards each.
   - **5–6 players:** 20 cards each.
3. Each player turns the **top card** of their Stock pile faceup, without looking at the cards beneath it.
4. Place the rest of the deck facedown in the center as the **Draw pile**. The Building piles and Discard piles start empty and are created through play.

## How to Play

Play begins with the person to the **left of the dealer** and proceeds clockwise.

**Your first turn:** Draw **5 cards** from the Draw pile to form your hand. Then play as many cards as you can and wish to:
- Start a Building pile with a **1** or a **Skip-Bo** card, or add to any Building pile in ascending sequence.
- You may play from your **hand** or from the **top of your Stock pile**.
- If you play all 5 cards from your hand, immediately draw 5 more and keep playing.

**Ending your turn:** When you cannot or choose not to play further, end your turn by **discarding one card** from your hand onto one of your four Discard piles.

**Later turns:** At the start of each turn, first draw enough cards to bring your hand back up to **5**. Then play cards onto the Building piles (always in sequence) from any of three sources: the **top of your Stock pile**, the **top of a Discard pile**, or your **hand**. End your turn with a discard as before.

**Key strategy:** Only emptying your Stock pile wins, so play from the top of your Stock pile whenever you can.

**If the Draw pile runs out:** gather the completed Building piles, shuffle them, and they become the new Draw pile.

## Scoring (Multi-Game)

To play a series, keep score. The winner of each game scores **5 points for every card remaining in each opponent's Stock pile**, plus **25 points** for winning. The first player to reach **500 points** wins.

## Partnership Play

With four players you may play in two partnerships. All rules stay the same, except:

- On your turn you may play from your own **Stock and Discard piles** *and* your **partner's**.
- Your partner must stay quiet during your turn—only the active player may ask a partner to make a play (e.g. "Partner, play your Skip-Bo as a 4" or "Partner, play your 7").
- Anyone caught coaching out of turn (cheating) takes **2 cards** from the Draw pile into their Stock pile, unseen.
- The game ends when **both** Stock piles of one partnership are emptied.
