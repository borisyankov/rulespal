# Scrabble Rulebook

## Table of Contents

*Scrabble is the classic crossword-style word game for 2 to 4 players. Draw lettered tiles and take turns building interlocking words across the board, angling to land your high-value letters on the premium squares that double and triple your score. Vocabulary, spatial cunning, and a bit of tile luck decide who comes out ahead. When the tiles run out and the last word is played, the highest score wins. This rulebook follows Hasbro's official rules.*

## Object of the Game

Score the most points by forming interlocking words — crossword fashion — on the board, using your lettered tiles to best advantage on the premium (colored) squares.

## Equipment

- Gameboard: a **15×15 grid**, with premium squares (Double/Triple Letter and Double/Triple Word).
- **100 letter tiles:** 98 lettered tiles plus **2 blank tiles**. Each letter has a printed point value; the blanks are worth zero.
- 4 racks; a tile bag.

**Tile distribution and values** (English set): A×9 (1), B×2 (3), C×2 (3), D×4 (2), E×12 (1), F×2 (4), G×3 (2), H×2 (4), I×9 (1), J×1 (8), K×1 (5), L×4 (1), M×2 (3), N×6 (1), O×8 (1), P×2 (3), Q×1 (10), R×6 (1), S×4 (1), T×6 (1), U×4 (1), V×2 (4), W×2 (4), X×1 (8), Y×2 (4), Z×1 (10), Blank×2 (0).

## Setup

1. Turn all tiles face down (or place in the bag) and mix them.
2. Each player draws one tile; the player with the letter **closest to "A"** plays first (a blank beats an A). Return the drawn tiles and remix.
3. Each player draws **7 tiles** and places them on their rack, hidden from opponents.

## Playing

Play passes to the left. On your turn choose **one** of three options: **place a word**, **exchange tiles**, or **pass**.

### Placing a Word

- The **first word** must be placed so that one of its tiles covers the **center star square**. It must be at least two letters and read left-to-right or top-to-bottom (never diagonally).
- On later turns, every new word must **connect** to tiles already on the board. You add one or more tiles to form a new word by:
  - Adding tiles to the front and/or back of a word already on the board;
  - Placing a word at right angles to a word on the board, using one of its letters;
  - Placing a complete word parallel to an existing word so that adjacent tiles also form complete words.
- **All** letters played in a line must form one complete word, and **every** new crossing sequence created by your tiles must also be a legitimate word.

After placing your word, announce your score, then **draw new tiles** to bring your rack back up to 7 (if any remain in the bag).

### Blanks

A blank tile may be used as **any letter**. When you play it, declare which letter it represents; it stays that letter for the rest of the game. A blank scores **zero points**, even on a letter-premium square, but the word premiums still apply to the rest of the word.

### Exchanging Tiles

Instead of placing a word, you may **exchange** any number of your tiles for new ones: discard the tiles face down, draw the same number, then return the discarded tiles to the bag. This uses your whole turn and scores nothing. You may only exchange if **at least 7 tiles remain** in the bag.

### Passing

You may forgo your turn and score nothing. **The game ends if all players pass twice in a row** (two consecutive scoreless turns each).

## Scoring

Sum the values of the letters in each word formed on your turn, applying premium squares:

- **Double / Triple Letter Score:** multiplies that single tile's value.
- **Double / Triple Word Score:** doubles or triples the value of the **entire word** using that square.
- **Letter premiums apply before word premiums.** If a word covers two word-premium squares, multiply again (e.g., two Double-Word squares = 4×).
- **A premium square counts only when a tile is first placed on it.** In later turns, tiles already on premiums score at face value.
- If you form **two or more words** in one turn (a word plus crossings), score **each** word; a shared letter counts (with its premium if newly covered) in every word that uses it.

### The 50-point Bonus ("Bingo")

If you place **all 7 of your tiles** in a single turn, you score a **50-point bonus**, added after the word's other points are totaled.

## End of the Game

The game ends when **all tiles have been drawn and one player uses their last tile** (emptying their rack), or when all possible plays are exhausted (all players pass twice).

**Final adjustment:**

- Each player **subtracts** from their score the total value of the tiles remaining on their rack.
- If a player used all their tiles ("went out"), they **add** the sum of all the other players' unplayed tiles to their own score.

The player with the **highest final score wins**. In a tie, the player with the highest score before adjustments wins (or the tie stands).

## Word Legality

Any word found in a standard dictionary is allowed, **except** words always capitalized, abbreviations, prefixes/suffixes standing alone, and words requiring an apostrophe or hyphen. A word may be **challenged** before the next player takes their turn: look it up — if it is invalid, the player removes those tiles and loses that turn; if valid, the challenger loses their next turn (rules vary by house/tournament convention).
