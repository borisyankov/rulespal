# Money! Rulebook

## Table of Contents

*Swap money with other players and build your position in different currencies. May the most cunning trader win!*

2-5 players | 15 minutes | Ages 11+

## Components

* **A** 1 Scoreboard
* **B** 1 Dry Erase Marker
* **C** 1 Market Board
* **D** 63 Money Cards *(7 currencies, 9 cards each)*
* **E** 12 Gold and Silver Cards *(6 cards each)*
* **F** 5 Fake Money Cards
* **G** 5 Player Aids

## Setup

1. Each player takes a 0-value fake money card and a player aid. Return unused fake money cards to the box.
2. With fewer than 5 players, remove money cards from the game as follows:
   * **2 or 4 Players:** Remove all cards of any 1 currency (9 cards). *See page 6 for 2-player rules.*
   * **3 Players:** Remove all cards of any 2 currencies (18 cards).
3. Shuffle all remaining money cards together with all gold and silver cards. Deal each player a hand of 6 cards. Each player adds their fake money card to their hand.
4. Place the deck on the market board in the middle of the table. To create the **market,** deal 2 faceup rows of 4 cards each to the left and right of the market board *(marked as "I" and "II").*
5. Place the scoreboard and marker to 1 side of the market.

## Game Rounds

The game is played in rounds. Each round has the 3 steps:

1. Place Bids
2. Player Turns
3. Round End

### 1. Place Bids

Players start each round by secretly selecting 1 or more cards from their hand to **bid.** Place the cards you want to bid facedown in front of you.

The **value** of your bid is the sum of the values of all cards in your bid *(including money, gold, and silver).*

When all bids have been placed, everyone reveals their bid by flipping their selected cards faceup.

### 2. Player Turns

Players take their turns in order from **highest to lowest bid.** On your turn, you must do one of the following:

* **A. Swap with the market:** Swap all cards in your bid with all cards on **one side** of the market board.
* **B. Swap with a player:** Swap all cards in your bid with all cards in another player's bid. *The cards you place in front of that player are now their bid.*
* **C. Keep:** Decline to swap and keep all cards in your bid.

After swapping or keeping your bid, take all cards in your bid **into your hand.** The player who has the highest bid left on the table takes their turn next.

*Note: After you swap with a player, that player will have the highest bid and take the next turn.*

### Ties

Whenever there is a tie for highest bid, each tied player checks the **serial numbers** on the cards in their bid. Whoever has the **lowest** serial number takes their turn first.

### Fake Money

Each player has a fake money card with **0 value** that they can use to hide how many "real" cards they bid. When bids are revealed, each player who reveals their fake money immediately returns it to hand.

### 3. Round End

When there are no more cards in front of players, the round ends. Any cards left in the market **stay there.**

Next, deal new faceup cards from the deck to **refill** each side of the market board up to 4 cards. If a side already has 4 or more cards, don't add any more. If there are not enough cards left in the deck to fill both sides, fill **side I** first, then **side II.** Then, start the next round.

## Game End

If the **last card** in the deck is dealt while refilling the market, play **1 more round,** then proceed to final scoring. *There will usually be fewer than 4 cards on one or both sides of the market board at the start of the last round.*

## Final Scoring

At the end of the game, each player reveals their hand and follows the steps on the next 2 pages to add up their final score. The player with the highest score wins. In a tie, victory is shared.

### Scoring Example

The rulebook illustrates final scoring with a sample player's completed scoreboard, listing a score for each of the 7 currencies (with 0 or blank scores for currencies in a weak position), a score for gold and silver, and a score for sets of 3. This player scores a total of 570 points.

### A. Score Currencies

For each currency, add up the **values** of all cards of that currency in your hand, then score as follows:

**Strong Position (200-300)**

If you have at least 200 of a currency, score points equal to the **total value** you have.

*Example: Total: 240 → Score: 240 points*

**Moderate Position (110-190)**

If you have more than 100 but less than 200 of a currency, you must pay a commission. **Subtract 100** from the total value you have and score points equal to the result.

*Example: Total: 140 → Score: 40 points*

**Weak Position (0-100)**

If you have 100 or less of a currency, it's worthless to you. Score 0 points.

*Example: Total: 60 → Score: 0 points*

### B. Score Gold & Silver

Add up the values of **all** your gold and silver cards and score points equal to the total.

*Example: Total: 90 → Score: 90 points*

### C. Score Sets of 3

Score a 100-point bonus for each set of 3 **exactly matching** cards you have. This includes:

* 100 per 3x **20s** of same currency
* 100 per 3x **30s** of same currency
* 100 per 3x **gold**
* 100 per 3x **silver**

*Example: Total: 2 sets → Score: 200 points*

## Tips & Reminders

* Do **not** count set bonuses when determining the highest bid or your position in a currency.
* Each currency has **3x** 20s, **3x** 30s, and **1x** each of 40, 50, and 60. Collecting all **9** cards of a currency is worth **500** points total *(300 value + 200 set bonuses).*
* You can score bonuses for sets of 20s or 30s of a currency even if you have a **weak position** in it.

## 2-Player Rules

Each player may use their **fake money** card to separate their selected cards into **2 bids** each round. The cards you reveal to the left and right of your fake money card are treated as separate bids. If you reveal cards on only 1 side of your fake money card, you only make 1 bid. Play progresses as usual, with each player taking 1 turn for **each** of their bids.
