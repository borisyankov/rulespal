# La Granja: Deluxe Master Set Farm Card Glossary

## Table of Contents

*This document lists all 126 farm cards in the game. Cards numbered 1-66 are the basic game cards, while cards 67-126 are all the cards added by the New Farm Cards module.*

## General Notes

Please keep the following in mind:

* Cards may be used immediately after being added to your farm.
* **Helper Timing:** The numbers on the bottom of the card title (A) indicate during which phase the card effect may be used. If two or more numbers are used, then the card effect can be used during any of those phases. If no number is present, then the card effect can be used at any time during your turn.
* **Helper Effect:** Some card effects can only be used once per game round. To indicate that a specific ability has been used, place an exhaustion marker on the card after applying its effects (B). At the end of the game round, return the exhaustion token to the general supply to show that the card effect can be used once again.
* When using a card effect, the effect must be resolved in full before another card effect can be triggered.

Explanation of the effects of some of the Helpers follows.

## B

**Banker** (F083, by Steph Hodge (All the Meeples of the Rainbow), Deluxe Master Set)

**Barrow Builder** (F009, Base Game) — You can retain the same market barrow from round to round if you choose to use this action on the same card every round. After completing the market barrow, the farm goods are discarded as normal, even if the market barrow itself is retained.

**Barterer** (F063, Base Game) — After completing a market barrow, you may return any 1 good to the corresponding den on your farm board.

**Basket Weaver** (F048, Base Game)

**Beekeeper** (F052, Base Game) — You may only count harvest goods on your fields, not in your dens.

**Blacksmith** (F064, Base Game)

**Book Keeper** (F099, by Nick Case, Deluxe Master Set) — Let's say you choose "olive". Count the number of olive symbols (including covered ones) on all market barrows currently attached to your farm. Take that many silver.

**Breeder** (F071, Promo)

**Brewer** (F055, Base Game) — As an anytime action, you may move grain from your fields to any of your harvest good dens (olives, grain, or grapes).

**Builder** (F025, Base Game) — The additional card must be played before new cards are drawn to refill your hand.

## C

**Cabinetmaker** (F050, Base Game) — You may take 1 pig even if you have no room for it. However, you must then immediately sell the pig.

**Candle Maker** (F031, by Julius Kündiger, Base Game) — After taking a die from revenue space #2 or #3 (and using it to obtain at least 1 harvest good) take 2 silver. *Note: The Bustling Town module provides different revenue benefits.*

**Card Player** (F092, by Matthias Nagy, Deluxe Master Set) — If you discard 2 cards you will draw 3 cards. However, you must discard at least 1 card to trigger the benefit.

**Carpenter** (F032, Base Game) — Let's say you played a card as your second farm extension, paying for it by spending 1 victory point and 1 silver. You may pay 1 additional different farm good (such as olive, grape, pig, etc. but not a victory point or silver) to play another card to your farm as either a market barrow, field, or helper. *Note: If you also have the Potter, the additional farm good must be different from all of goods used to pay for the extension.*

**Chandler** (F113, by Julius Kündiger, Deluxe Master Set) — Each opponent marker only counts once. Thus, if an opponent marker is adjacent to two of your markers, it should only be counted once.

**Chef** (F001, Base Game) — You could upgrade grapes into wine for 2 silver instead of 3.

**Contractor** (F087, by Tantrum House, Deluxe Master Set) — The effect of this card is added to the normal cost. If you buy the marked roof tile, you do not pay the extra cost.

**Cooper** (F018, Base Game) — The grape represents the cost to make the delivery. It cannot also be used as an item to be delivered (though a different grape could be delivered). *Note that the grape must come from one of your fields. You cannot spend grapes from a den or buy, using the appropriate anytime action.*

**Countrywoman** (F104, by Kevin Hendranata, Deluxe Master Set) — Be careful with this effect so you still have a farm card in hand at the beginning of the next round.

**Craftworker** (F101, by Kevin Hendranata, Deluxe Master Set) — The good that will be upgraded can be purchased via an anytime action as usual.

**Creature of Habit** (F122, by ode., Deluxe Master Set)

**Customs Official** (F109, by Julius Kündiger, Deluxe Master Set)

**Dealer** (F080, by David Waybright (Man vs Meeple), Deluxe Master Set)

**Deer Breeder** (F036, Base Game) — Only count spaces from farm extensions. Do not count any spaces provided by helpers (such as the Groom or Swineherd).

**Donkey Breeder** (F081, by Heavy Cardboard, Deluxe Master Set) — The craft marker from building 2 may be taken into account when using its permanent side.

**Donkey Driver** (F029, Base Game)

**"Donkey Train" Spotter** (F091, by Tony Boydell, Deluxe Master Set)

**Drover** (F118, by ode., Deluxe Master Set)

## E

**Entrepreneur** (F082, by The Brothers Murph, Deluxe Master Set)

**Errand Boy** (F047, Base Game) — There are 3 kinds of harvest goods—olives, grain, and grapes.

**Expert Farmhand** (F075, by David Waybright (Man vs Meeple), Deluxe Master Set) — The activated helper must be able to act during the same Phase. The helper must also not be marked as used this round.

## F

**Farm Girl** (F019, Base Game) — If this card is lost by any means, the card limit is immediately decreased as well. If you are currently holding more cards than you are allowed to, immediately discard any excess.

**Farm Servant** (F023, Base Game) — As an anytime action, you may perform a free upgrade each round.

**Farm Shop Operator** (F120, by ode., Deluxe Master Set)

**Farm Worker** (F004, Base Game) — As an anytime action, you may spend 1 victory point to play a card to your farm. If you play the card as a farm extension, the standard costs must be paid as usual. The spent victory point is not considered part of those costs.

**Farmyard Trader** (F068, Promo) — If you spend 1 olive you will be taking 1 grain and 1 grape.

**Fat Cat** (F060, Base Game) — Let's say you took a die from revenue space #4. You would take a total of 6 silver—4 silver per standard rules and 2 additional silver for having this card in play. *Note: The Bustling Town module provides different revenue benefits.*

**Fodder Maid** (F041, Base Game) — You may spend 1 grain to take 1 pig even if you have no room for it. However, you must then immediately sell the pig (effectively giving you 3 silver for the grain instead of 1). *Note: The grain must come from one of your fields. You cannot spend grain from a den or by using the appropriate anytime action.*

**Foreman** (F014, Base Game) — This effect is triggered every time another player takes a revenue die of the same value as a revenue (including Ana's die and grey dice) on your farm board. If the 3rd die matches, take 1 silver for each opponent (i.e. in a 4-player game, you would receive 3 silver). *Note: This effect also triggers when other effects allow a player to "use" or "reuse" a die (such as the Peon). It does not, however, get triggered when other effects allow a player the use of a revenue space without taking a corresponding die (such as the Merchant).*

## G

**Gambler** (F090, by Heavy Cardboard, Deluxe Master Set) — Revenue spaces 1 and 6 are considered adjacent.

**Gardener** (F049, Base Game) — You must already have a harvest good on a field or in a den to use this ability. Each marker may be used for a harvest good of your choice.

**Gourmet Chef** (F121, by ode., Deluxe Master Set)

**Greengrocer** (F028, Base Game)

**Groom** (F012, Base Game) — This card provides an additional space for a pig. *Note: If you also have the Deer Breeder, this space does not count as an empty space.*

## H

**Haggler** (F059, Base Game) — Let's say you completely supplied the wainwright craft building. You may return 1 food, wine, or cured meat from the craft building to the corresponding den on your farm board.

**Hard Worker** (F098, by Shei Santos, Deluxe Master Set)

**Harvest Hand** (F034, Base Game) — You must choose two different types of fields (such as an olive field and a grain field, but not two olive fields). This effect allows a field to hold more than 1 harvest good.

**Helpmate** (F093, by MiKe, Deluxe Master Set) — For example, when completing craft building #1 (six silver) you can pay 1 silver to gain 1 pig. *Note: The Bustling Town module provides different revenue benefits.*

**Herder** (F045, Base Game)

**Hermit** (F117, by Julius Kündiger, Deluxe Master Set) — All adjacent spaces need to be free.

## I

**Instructor** (F056, Base Game)

**Investor** (F078, by Jon Gets Games, Deluxe Master Set)

## J

**Joiner** (F022, Base Game) — You may spend upgraded goods to help pay the cost of a roof marker, either in part or in full. Any overpayment is lost.

## L

**Lender** (F084, by Filip Głowacz, Deluxe Master Set) — You still take the marker from the building you fully supplied and will use the flipside of that marker for the remainder of the game.

**Long Distance Trader** (F066, Base Game) — Trade commodities may be exchanged for certain goods and actions. When using this ability to take 2 victory points, use a player marker to mark your chosen goods or action. For the remainder of the game, you may not choose marked goods or actions when exchanging trade commodities. It is possible for you to earn up to 10 points from this card.

## M

**Manager** (F096, by Rebecca Zeeb, Deluxe Master Set) — Remember that you can only replace helpers if all your helper slots are full. *Note that the effect of the Manager will also trigger when using the helper ability of the Actress or Renovator. It will not trigger, however, when using the helper effect of the Registrar or the Manager.*

**Manservant** (F030, Base Game) — If this card is lost by any means, the card limit is immediately decreased as well. If you are currently holding more cards than you are allowed to, immediately discard any excess.

**Market Advisor** (F097, by Isra Cendrero, Deluxe Master Set) — This free delivery is carried out during the free delivery step. Every craft building has a die symbol depicted next to it on the main game board.

**Market Woman** (F021, Base Game) — For example, if you exchange a trade commodity for 1 pig, you may immediately spend 3 silver to perform 2 upgrades, draw or play a card, take 2 different harvest goods, or take 4 silver — but you could not take another pig.

**Marketer** (F042, by MiKe, Base Game) — You can trigger this effect by taking a die from revenue space #2 (and using it to draw or play a card) or #5 (and using it to advance at least 1 step on the siesta track). *Note: The Bustling Town module provides different revenue benefits.*

**Master Trader** (F079, by Our Family Plays Games, Deluxe Master Set)

**Matriarch** (F102, by Kevin Hendranata, Deluxe Master Set)

**Merchant** (F006, Base Game) — During the first game round, this would be revenue space #1. During the second game round, this is revenue space #2, etc. The benefit is received in addition to the standard revenue dice taken that round.

**Migrant Laborer** (F015, Base Game) — If both the Migrant Laborer and the Tenant are in play, the Tenant effect is resolved first. After the Tenant chooses a die, the Migrant Laborer may turn a different die.

**Mobster** (F116, by Julius Kündiger, Deluxe Master Set) — Even if you remove multiple player markers, you receive exactly 1 trade commodity and 0 VP total for the removed markers.

**Monger** (F008, Base Game) — You receive exactly 1 additional VP, regardless of how far up the siesta track you are, as long as you score at least 1 VP from the siesta track.

## N

**Negotiator** (F002, Base Game) — The victory points earned from this effect are awarded immediately. You receive a victory point if your marker is ultimately removed from the market (not simply moved), even if you remove it yourself. If multiple markers are removed, you earn a victory point for each marker.

**Nice Neighbor** (F123, by MiKe, Deluxe Master Set)

## O

**Oil Miller** (F017, Base Game) — As an anytime action, you may spend 1 olive to play a card to your farm. If you play the card as a farm extension, the standard costs must be paid as usual. The spent olive is not considered part of those costs. *Note that the olive must come from one of your fields. You cannot spend olive from a den or by using the appropriate anytime action.*

**Olive Picker** (F053, Base Game) — As an anytime action, you may move olives from your fields to any of your harvest good dens (olives, grain, or grapes).

**Organizer** (F125, by MiKe, Deluxe Master Set) — The discarded die is taken from a revenue space as normal, but is placed next to the game board without effect. The die cannot be taken for the remainder of the round.

**Ornament Carver** (F054, Base Game)

## P

**Packer** (F020, Base Game) — This free delivery is carried out during the paid delivery step.

**Peddler** (F033, Base Game) — The marker must be moved to an immediately adjacent, empty space. If no such space exists, the marker is removed as normal. In any case, victory points are scored normally. The relocated marker does not remove any other markers.

**Peon** (F013, Base Game) — First, take a revenue die and resolve its effect. Immediately thereafter, you may activate the effect of this helper. You may not, however, reuse the effect of your 1st die after taking your 2nd die. For example, if you just took a "1", you may spend 1 victory point to take a second pig. If this card was put into play via a revenue space, its effect does not apply to the current action. *Note: The Bustling Town module provides different revenue benefits.*

**Picker** (F070, Promo) — Place the harvest good into the corresponding den.

**Pig Breeder** (F062, Base Game) — As long as you have at least 3 pigs and at least 2 empty spaces for pigs, receive 2 offspring. If you do not have sufficient space to hold the pigs, then you may not use this benefit (you may not immediately sell the offspring). *Note: If you have 5 total spaces and 4 or 5 pigs, you could sell 1 or 2 pigs as an anytime action to free up the necessary room to use this helper ability. Note: This ability is not affected by the Breeder ability.*

**Pig Harness Maker** (F086, by Tony Boydell, Deluxe Master Set)

**Pig Herder** (F126, by MiKe, Deluxe Master Set)

**Pitchman** (F046, Base Game) — Whenever you remove an opponent's marker, receive 2 victory points in total—1 victory point per standard rules and 1 additional victory point for having this card in play.

**Planning Economist** (F111, by Julius Kündiger, Deluxe Master Set)

**Ploughboy** (F058, Base Game)

**Porter** (F003, Base Game) — If you take a die from revenue space 6, you would receive 1 free delivery and 2 silver. When taking a die from revenue space 2, you may choose 2 of the 4 indicated options. The "draw or play a card" revenue effect is considered a single option. You cannot choose to draw and play a card. If this card was put into play via a revenue space, its effect does not apply to the current action. *Note: The Bustling Town module provides different revenue benefits.*

**Potter** (F038, Base Game) — You may use any combination of farm goods when paying for farm extensions. For example, you could spend 2 silver to pay for your second extension, or 2 grain and 1 victory point to pay for your third extension. *Note: If you also have the Carpenter, the additional farm good (from the Carpenter's ability) must be different from all of goods used to pay for the extension.*

**Prime Deliverer** (F094, by ode., Deluxe Master Set)

**Puffer** (F107, by Kevin Hendranata, Deluxe Master Set) — If you are completing a market barrow of value 3, this means you may pay 1 upgraded good to place your marker onto any space with a value of 5 (as if the market barrow was of value 5). In this case, any adjacent lower-valued opponent markers would not be removed. When completing a market barrow of value 5, this ability can still be used to place your marker on a space with a value of 6 (but otherwise you may not increase the value by just 1). This effect cannot be used if empty higher-valued spaces do not exist. This ability has no effect on market barrows of value 6.

## R

**Refiner** (F095, by Martin Zeeb, Deluxe Master Set) — After upgrading 1 olive into 1 food, you could, for example, also upgrade 1 pig into 1 cured meat.

**Registrar** (F115, by Julius Kündiger, Deluxe Master Set) — This allows you to have 2 helpers in one slot. The second helper does not take up a slot of its own.

**Renovator** (F119, by ode., Deluxe Master Set) — If the discarded card was played as a farm extension you do not need to pay for it.

**Roofer** (F040, Base Game) — Olives, grain, grapes, and pigs are the resources in the game. The resource or resources may be delivered either to a craft building or to one of your market barrows.

## S

**Sausage Maker** (F074, by Jon Gets Games, Deluxe Master Set)

**Seeds Trader** (F044, Base Game) — For example, if you take a die from revenue space #4 you would take 3 silver and 1 harvest good (instead of 4 silver). *Note: The Bustling Town module provides different revenue benefits.*

**Seller** (F106, by Kevin Hendranata, Deluxe Master Set) — You could, for example, pay 1 silver, 1 olive, and 1 grain to take 1 pig and 1 victory point.

**Shack Builder** (F010, Base Game) — If you have this helper in play, the first farm extension would be free, your second extension would cost 1 farm good, the third 2 farm goods, etc. You do not receive any retroactive benefits for extensions built prior to acquiring this helper.

**Shingle Maker** (F069, Promo)

**Shoemaker** (F061, Base Game)

**Shopkeeper** (F077, by Tantrum House, Deluxe Master Set) — If you get 0 victory points from both the market and the siesta track, you still gain 1 victory point from this card. If you receive 3 victory points and 2 victory points, you receive 1 silver.

**Silk Spinner** (F124, by MiKe, Deluxe Master Set)

**Silver Smith** (F073, by Kevin Hendranata, Deluxe Master Set)

**Skilled Worker** (F088, by Our Family Plays Games, Deluxe Master Set)

**Snorer** (F100, by Matthias Nagy, Deluxe Master Set) — This free delivery is carried out after the paid delivery step. At the end of the round, the markers are arranged in turn order as normal.

**Stall Builder** (F039, Base Game) — If you are fully supplying a market barrow of value 3, this means you may place your marker onto any space with a value of 4 (as if the market barrow was of value 4). In this case, any adjacent lower-valued opponent markers would not be removed. This effect cannot be used if empty higher-valued spaces do not exist. This ability has no effect on market barrows of value 6.

**Storage Builder** (F043, Base Game) — This helper gives you one additional space for a roof tile, allowing you to purchase up to six roof tiles. You do not have to use up your normal roof spaces first. When building a roof tile here, immediately receive 3 victory points. Once a roof tile has been built on this card, the Storage Builder can no longer be lost or discarded by any means.

**Straw Binder** (F037, Base Game)

**Supplier** (F065, Base Game)

**Swineherd** (F051, by MiKe, Base Game) — This card provides additional spaces for 2 pigs. *Note: If you also have the Deer Breeder, this space does not count as an empty stable.*

## T

**Taxidermist** (F085, by Tony Boydell, Deluxe Master Set)

**Tenant** (F026, Base Game) — If both the Tenant and the Migrant Laborer are in play, the Tenant effect is resolved first. After the Tenant chooses a die, the Migrant Laborer may turn a different die. Farm goods are placed into their respective dens, as usual.

**Thief** (F114, by Julius Kündiger, Deluxe Master Set)

**Thimblerigger** (F108, by Kevin Hendranata, Deluxe Master Set) — If activating this helper during the Revenue Phase: after rolling the die and resolving this helper effect, return the die to its original value and location.

**Thrifty Helper** (F089, by Steph Hodge (All the Meeples of the Rainbow), Deluxe Master Set)

**Trader** (F007, Base Game)

**Traveling Grocer** (F067, Promo)

**Turner** (F011, Base Game) — If you have this helper in play, the roof tile would be free during the first game round. During the second game round, the roof tile would cost 1 silver, etc. You do not receive any retroactive benefits for roof tiles built prior to acquiring this helper.

## V

**Village Elder** (F005, Base Game) — This card prevents opponents from taking the last revenue die of a given number. They can still take dice from that revenue space, as long as at least one die remains. If this die remains as the last die not taken, all players receive the benefit from that revenue space as normal (the 3rd die).

**Vintner** (F057, Base Game) — As an anytime action, you may move grapes from your fields to any of your harvest good dens (olives, grain, or grapes).

## W

**Wagoner** (F024, Base Game) — Let's say you have 3 farm extensions that provide paid delivery options and choose to use all 3 of them. The first one is free, after which you receive 1 silver. You may use that silver to pay for your second delivery and another silver to pay for the third delivery. Effectively, during the paid delivery step, you can perform 2 paid deliveries at no cost.

**Wagoner Servant** (F027, Base Game)

**Wainwright** (F016, Base Game) — The new card must be played as a market barrow. Wainwright and Barrow Builder may be used on the same barrow.

**Warehouseman** (F105, by Kevin Hendranata, Deluxe Master Set)

**Warrior** (F112, by Julius Kündiger, Deluxe Master Set) — If you are completing a market barrow of value 3, this means you may remove one adjacent opponent marker of value 4. You may still remove lower-valued opponent markers as normal. This ability has no effect on market barrows of value 6.
