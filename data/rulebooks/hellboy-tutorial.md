# Hellboy: The Board Game Tutorial

## Table of Contents

*Tutorial - Read This First!*

*Welcome to Hellboy: The Board Game! This booklet is designed to make your transition from know-nothing rookie to veteran B.P.R.D. Agent as smooth as possible. It will play you through the first few rounds of a game, showing you how the basics work – you can read along, but we'd recommend that you set things up and play along as well. Either way, when you're done you can turn to the Rulebook and get a proper understanding of the full game, and then investigate your first case!*

*In a game of Hellboy, each player normally controls one B.P.R.D. Agent (or two, if you're playing solo), but this tutorial features two Agents regardless of the number of players, to keep things quick and simple. If you have more than two players, you could take turns controlling the Agents or team up to control them together. The tutorial should only last around 20 minutes, and the aim of it is to learn the gameplay rather than to 'win'. Follow the actions made in the examples that follow, and once you're comfortable you can take over yourself and finish the game.*

*Introductions aside – let's get started!*

## Getting Ready

Open the Rulebook to page 4, where you can see a guide to all the components referred to during this tutorial. Punch out the HQ board, room tiles, counters and markers, and separate the cards into their decks by type.

**Important:** Do not open the Case File decks yet.

### The Case File Deck

The Case File decks are the beating heart of the game and need to be handled with a little reverence – it's vital that they're not shuffled or reordered before you play, and it's even more important that you don't peek at what's inside them! If you accidentally drop them or inadvertently shuffle them, the best thing you can do is find someone who isn't going to play the game and ask them nicely to put the cards back in order. They should be arranged by set (using the icon in the bottom-left corner), in numerical order as shown by the numbers beneath the icon.

The Case File deck should be treated as a whole object – if a player wants to read the top card more clearly, they should move the whole deck and not just the top card. If the top card is flipped, it still stays on top of the deck. The only exception to this rule is when the top card is discarded, at which point it is moved to the left of the HQ board and is no longer in play.

Find the "Eviction Notice" Case File (the one with the top card shown above) and put it in the middle of the playing area. The others can go back in the box for now.

## Setting Up

### Case Files

The front of each Case File deck gives you a short briefing – this is all the information you'll have going into the mission.

Read the briefing on the front of Eviction Notice aloud so you and your fellow players know what you're up against.

### HQ Board

Set up the HQ board to one side of the playing area, as shown to the right. The Impending Doom and Information Gathered markers are each placed on the first space of their track at the top of the board, and Hellboy and Abe's Target Priority markers are placed on the Target Priority queue, and the Case File is put on its space.

### Agents

Then, prepare the two **Agents**, Hellboy and Abe Sapien. For each, find their **miniature**, **Agent board and starting cards** (*Hellboy's Pistol* and *Deep Pockets* for Hellboy, and *Abe Sapien's Sidearm* and *Harpoon* for Abe). Also, grab a set of three **action cubes** and a **base clip** of the same colour for each Agent (attach the clip to the base of the miniature so it's easy to keep track of who's who). Set everything up as shown here.

### Deck of Doom

Take the **Deck of Doom** and go through the cards, looking at the icons in the top-left corner of each. All cards with one of the icons shown to the right are shuffled together to form the Deck of Doom for this game; any cards with other icons are returned to the box. Put the Deck of Doom on its space on the HQ board.

### Encounter Deck

Then take the **Encounter deck** and go through it in the same way. In this case, you're only looking for three cards – the ones that have a small white hexagon next to their icon (found in the bottom left corner). Shuffle these together to form the Encounter deck, and put it on its space on the HQ board.

### The Game Board

Finally, set up the board as shown to the right. This uses a total of four **room tiles**, three **doors** (A) and the **Starting Area marker**, as well as the Agent miniatures.

The three cards from the Encounter deck should be placed into the rooms as shown, and then flipped face-down. Normally, the order of these cards is random and the players won't know what they'll find in each room until the Agents explore it – we're just keeping things simple for the tutorial.

A few notes about the board – each separate tile is called a **room** (B), and each square space within a room is called an **area** (C). This board contains one room with a single area, two rooms with two areas each and one room with four areas.

With this done, discard the top card of the Case File deck. You will note that the back of this card contains the setup instructions that we just talked you through above.

Whenever a Case File card is flipped or discarded - like you just did - it reveals a new Case File card, which should be read and resolved immediately. Do this now! This card tells you to put insight markers on spaces 2, 4 and 8 of the Information Gathered track. It also tells you to put a **trigger marker** on space VII of the Impending Doom track as there are two Agents, and a matching **reaction marker** on the card. Finally, you should move the card to the in play area (the area above the HQ board). Any cards here are **in play**, just like the top card of the Case File deck, and will have an ongoing effect on the game.

Moving the card to this area reveals another new Case File card, which instructs you to move it straight to the in-play area. Do this now – you will reveal another new card, which should again be resolved immediately and left in place on the HQ board. There's some flavour text, followed by an instruction to put some **Enemy cards** into the **Minion slots** beneath the HQ board. This completes the setup – everything should now look like it does below.

## Your Objective

Each game of Hellboy ends with a Confrontation, a climactic showdown against a Boss. As the briefing explained, your mission is to hunt down a really big frog monster – so no prizes for guessing which Boss you're facing!

There are three ways the **Confrontation** can begin.

- The worst way is if all Agents get **knocked out** at the same time and wake to see the Boss approaching.
- The most common way for the Confrontation to trigger is if the Impending Doom marker reaches the trigger marker on its track.
- In some Case Files, there's a more advantageous way to reach the Boss - the Agents will need to gain the element of surprise by exploring the board as quickly as possible and finding a secret entrance, if there is one…

Each of these outcomes will cause a Case File card to be flipped or discarded. This will give instructions for starting the Confrontation.

## Playing the Game

A game of *Hellboy* proceeds through a number of **rounds**, each broken into five **phases**:

Enemy Phase / Agent Phase / Rest Phase / Doom Phase / End Phase

Each one does pretty much what you'd expect: Enemies act in the Enemy phase, Agents act in the Agent phase, you can rest in the Rest phase, bad things happen in the Doom phase and the End phase is for tidying up at the end of the round. As soon as a round ends, a new one begins.

Now that you're ready to begin the game, you should close the rulebook and leave it to one side with the back cover in view. This page contains a summary of all the key rules in case you need a reminder. You can refer to it as we go through the examples, and you'll find page references to the main rules in case you want to read up on anything in more detail.

Our first round begins with the **Enemy phase**, but there are no Enemies on the board for now so we'll skip it until round two.

### Round 1 – Agent Phase

The **Agent phase** is the real meat of the game. Here, players can spend their three **action cubes** to make actions. They can do this in any order they like – there's no turn order between the players. Abe could spend all three followed by Hellboy, or they could take turns spending one each. It's up to the players, and they can do it differently every round. There are also **free actions**, which don't cost cubes, and we're gonna start with one of those.

Abe makes a free **Explore** action to check out the first room. The Encounter card is flipped and we see that its information is split across four boxes. The first two are set up in the closest area, and the other two are set up in the furthest area. This card shows two Enemies. Minion A means an Enemy from the Minion A slot under the HQ board – so here, we have a Rampaging Frog Monster and a Venomous Frog Monster. The room also contains a clue, which will be key to advancing the Information Gathered track and collecting those insights.

There's now a Frog Monster in front of Abe, so he kicks things off with a **Shoot** action with his sidearm, spending an action cube to do so. A lot of actions, Shoot included, require a **test**, where the player rolls dice to determine how successful they are. You always roll three **test dice** plus the **effect die**. Here, Abe rolls the red test dice as his **SHOOT** skill level is red as seen in the top left corner of his **Agent board**. He scores a total of **6**.

This would normally inflict 6 **damage** on the Frog Monster, but looking at its **Enemy card** it has a **Resilience** of 2, which reduces the total score to 4. Abe's player puts four **damage markers** next to the Frog Monster on the board to show the damage it's suffered.

Abe spends another action cube to make a second Shoot action, rolling 2, 1 and 1 on the test dice and the **double** symbol on the effect die. This time, the effect die doubles the highest single test die score (from 2 to 4), giving a score of 6. That's another four damage on the Frog Monster. As soon as an Enemy's total damage equals or beats its **Health** it is defeated and removed from the board. The Frog Monster's Health is 6, so this is enough to defeat it.

Now Hellboy decides to step up. He spends an action cube to make a **Move** action, letting him move up to two areas.

He uses this to get into the same area as the Venomous Frog Monster, and then makes a **Fight** action with his second action cube. This is resolved just like a Shoot action but targets Enemies in the Agent's area, and uses the **FIGHT** skill level. Hellboy's FIGHT is red. Wanting to make sure he defeats his target, Hellboy spends his third action cube at the same time to **boost** his roll, **upgrading** one of his dice from red (the highest possible skill level, with a maximum score of 3) to black (only available through upgrading, with a maximum score of 4). He scores an awesome 7 on the test dice, and rolls the **B.P.R.D.** symbol on the effect die.

This symbol is a wild card, and can be used as any other result of the player's choice. He uses it to double the black die's score to 6, giving a total score of 10. Even taking off the Minion's Resilience of 2, that's still 8 damage – more than enough to defeat the Frog Monster.

Boom! That's both Enemies dealt with.

Abe's got one action cube left, so he makes a **Move** action to catch up with Hellboy. This ends the Agent phase.

### Round 1 – Other Phases

The **Rest phase** is optional, so we'll skip it for now, but we'll cover it later.

In the **Doom phase** the top card is drawn from the Deck of Doom, resolved, and then discarded to the right of the HQ board.

In our example, the players draw Time's A-Wastin'… which advances the Impending Doom track (moving the marker from the I space to the II space), and brings the game closer to the deadly Confrontation!

In the End phase each player returns their three action cubes to their Agent board. Then a new round begins!

As the Agents did such a good job clearing out the Frog Monsters, again there are no Enemies on the board, so it's straight on to the Agent phase.

### Round 2 – Agent Phase

Abe takes the lead again, opening with an **Examine** action in an attempt to claim the clue counter in their area. His **EXAMINE** skill level is orange, so he rolls three orange dice plus the test die, and scores a measly 2.

Examine actions need a score of at least 3 to be successful, so this isn't enough to obtain any information. Abe tries again, spending a second cube, and uses his third and final cube to boost and upgrade one of his dice to red.

Hellboy, keen to investigate the clue and get moving, also spends an action cube to **assist** on the test. This has the same effect as a boost, upgrading another die to red, so that's now two red dice and one orange die.

This time, Abe scores 6 (thanks to another double on the effect die), which is enough to discard the clue counter and advance the Information Gathered marker by not 1, but 2! He takes the insight marker from the '2' space and places it on his Agent board for later.

Hellboy's itching to get going, so he makes a free **Explore** action to check out the next room. Flipping the Encounter card, this reveals another Venomous Frog Monster, another clue, a Frog Swarm, and a size-2 scenery piece (the actual piece doesn't matter, as long as the number on the scenery counter is a 2).

Seeing the new Enemy in front of him, he uses his second cube to take aim with his pistol – knowing it could potentially kill the Frog Monster in a single shot – and (of course) rolls terribly, causing no damage.

The *Hellboy's Pistol* card is flipped after it's been fired, meaning it's out of ammo and needs reloading. He uses his last action cube to move into the Frog Monster's area.

### Round 2 – Other Phases

The Agents can't rest while there are Enemies on the board, so the Rest phase is skipped again.

In the Doom phase the *Unexpected Attack* card is drawn from the Deck of Doom, advancing Impending Doom again and introducing another Venomous Frog Monster into Abe's space. Uh oh!

In the End phase the Agents get their cubes back. Frog Swarms are also resolved in this phase, but only when they are in an area with an Agent, so the Frog Swarm will do nothing for now, and it's on to the third round.

### Round 3 – Enemy Phase

This time, there are Enemies on the board for the Enemy Phase. If an Enemy is in an Agent's area it makes a **Melee attack** against it, which is what will happen here.

In our example, Hellboy and Abe are in separate areas. However, if they had been in the same area and were attacked, that's where the target priority queue comes into play. The Agent at the front of the queue is the one that gets attacked, and then their marker is moved to the back of the queue instead.

When an Agent is attacked they make a **DEFENCE test** – this is the same as a FIGHT, SHOOT, or EXAMINE test but uses their DEFENCE skill level.

Hellboy rolls first, scoring 1, 2 and 2 on his test dice and a **catastrophe** on the effect die. This result removes his highest-scoring die, giving him a score of 3. The Enemy's **Melee Attack** value (shown on its Enemy card) is 5, and Hellboy takes **damage** for each point by which it exceeds his Defence score – that's two damage. For each damage an Agent suffers a **damage marker** is taken at random and placed into one of the boxes of their **health track** (at the bottom of their Agent board) with its **wound** side showing, as seen in the image. The **catastrophe** on the effect die has an additional effect in this case thanks to the Venomous Frog Monster's special rule – its paralysing tongue causes Hellboy to discard one of his action cubes.

Abe does a little better. Even though his DEFENCE skill is only yellow, he manages to score 4, meaning he only takes a single damage marker.

### Round 3 – Agent Phase

Hellboy wants revenge for the damage he just suffered, so in the Agent phase he spends his two action cubes (remember that he lost one to the Venomous Frog Monster) for a **Big Right Hook** action - one of the Unique Actions on his Agent card, only available to him. He rolls well and with the 3 bonus damage from his Big Right Hook he defeats his attacker in one hit.

Abe's equally fortunate, using a **Fight** action to take out the Frog Monster that jumped him. He uses the other two actions to Move into the next room and Examine the clue, scoring 5 which lets him advance the Information Gathered track by 1, and claim a second insight marker from the '3' space.

### Round 3 – Other Phases

This round the Agents decide to use the Rest phase. This can be done whenever there are no Enemies around, but when they do the Impending Doom marker advances, so be careful!

First, Abe's *Rapid Healing* special rule on his Agent board lets him heal the damage he's suffered when the Agents decide to rest. (*Rapid Healing: Whenever the Agents take time, Abe heals 2 damage.*)

Then, each Agent can move to any area of the board and make a special **rest action**.

Both Agents move to the area with the closed door, ready to Explore in the next round. Then, Hellboy takes the chance to **Recuperate**, healing his damage, while Abe chooses the Secure action and clears out the Frog Swarm in the room.

Frog Swarms don't do much by themselves, but letting them build up is a bad idea as they will increase the Impending Doom track!

Once all rest actions are complete, the Impending Doom track advances. Normally this would end the phase, but one of the in-play Case File cards says "If the Agents take time, flip this card immediately after advancing the Impending Doom tracker." Do this now and follow the instructions shown. With this done, the phase ends.

The Doom phase reveals the Bad Temper Doom card. This affects Hellboy, meaning one of his action cubes can only be used to make **Fight** actions each round. At least the card doesn't advance the Impending Doom track – just a few spaces until the Confrontation!

The End phase lets the Agents get their cubes back, and this ends the third round… and the tutorial.

Now you've got an idea of how the basics work, you can either finish playing this Case File or start afresh. If something comes up that hasn't been covered, read through the Rulebook to familiarise yourself with the rules before continuing. You'll pick it up in no time.

*Best of luck!*
