# Mancala Rulebook

## Table of Contents

*Mancala is one of the oldest known family of counting-and-capturing games, played for thousands of years across Africa and Asia. This rulebook covers Kalah, the version most commonly sold commercially as "Mancala." Two players sow seeds (or stones) around a board of pits, trying to gather the most seeds in their own store. For 2 players.*

## Object

Collect more seeds in your store than your opponent by the time the game ends.

## Equipment

- **1 board** with two rows of **6 small pits** and a larger **store** (called a *Kalah*, or *mancala*) at each end.
- **48 seeds** (stones, beads, or marbles).

## Setup

Place the board between the two players so that each player has a row of 6 pits closest to them, with their **store** on their **right-hand end**.

Place **4 seeds** in each of the 12 small pits. The two stores start empty.

Each player "owns" the 6 pits on their side of the board and the store to their right.

## How to Play

Players take turns. On your turn, **sow** the seeds from one of your own pits:

1. Choose one of the 6 pits on **your** side that contains at least one seed. Scoop up **all** the seeds from that pit.
2. Moving **counterclockwise**, drop one seed into each pit you pass, one at a time.
3. **Include your own store** when passing it, dropping a seed in. **Skip your opponent's store**—never place a seed there.
4. Continue past your opponent's pits (dropping seeds in them too) if you still have seeds in hand, looping around the board as needed.

## Special Rules

**Free turn.** If the **last seed** you sow lands in **your own store**, you immediately **take another turn**. You may earn several extra turns in a row this way.

**Capture.** If the **last seed** you sow lands in an **empty pit on your own side**, you **capture**: take that last seed *and* all the seeds in the **opposite pit** (your opponent's pit directly across from it) and place them all in **your store**. If the opposite pit is empty, no capture happens—your seed simply stays there.

## Ending the Game

The game ends as soon as **one player's six pits are all empty** on their turn (they have no legal move to make).

When this happens, the **other player** moves all the seeds remaining in their own six pits into their own store.

Then each player counts the seeds in their store. **The player with the most seeds wins.** An equal count (24 each) is a tie.

## Strategy Notes

- Setting up a chain of moves that repeatedly ends in your store lets you take many turns in a row.
- Watch for capture opportunities—emptying one of your pits so a later move lands its last seed there can net a big haul from the pit opposite.
- Track your opponent's threats too: leaving many seeds in a pit opposite one of their empty pits invites a capture.
