# Expeditions Rulebook

## Table of Contents

*Expeditions is a competitive, card-driven, tile-exploration, engine-building game set in an alternate-history 1920s, a standalone sequel to Scythe. Years after a mysterious meteorite crashed in the Siberian tundra near Tunguska, a strange corruption has spread across the land. Fresh crews of adventurers, each pairing a heroic character with a loyal animal companion and a walking diesel-powered mech, set out to explore the changed wilderness, recover cards and artifacts, cleanse the corruption with power and guile, and return home with the greatest glory. For 1-5 players, ages 14+, about 60-90 minutes.*

## Overview and Goal

Over the course of the game you build a personal engine of cards while your mech explores a shared map of hex-shaped locations. You play cards to generate two resources, **Power** and **Guile**, and to unlock **worker** abilities; you move your mech to mysterious locations to gain more cards and tokens; you enhance your engine with **items**, **meteorites**, and **quests**; and you spend Power and Guile to **vanquish** the corruption that covers many locations.

Along the way you earn **glory** by reaching notable milestones. The moment a player places their fourth glory token, the end of the game is triggered. At game end, your accumulated money (coins) plus the value of your glory tokens, vanquished corruption, upgraded items, and solved quests are totaled. The player with the most money wins.

## Components

- 1 central map of hex location tiles (arranged in three zones: Southern, Central, and Northern)
- 1 Basecamp board showing the glory categories and glory track
- Player mechs (one per color), each in a matching plastic base
- Dual-layer mech mats (player mats) used to track resources, workers, upgrades, solved quests, and melded meteorites
- Character cards, each with a matching animal companion card
- A main deck of cards (mechs/items/meteorites/quests found while exploring)
- Worker meeples in several colors
- Corruption tokens (in Power and Guile colors, each printed with a value), plus one high-value corruption tile
- Map tokens (placed on the face-down locations)
- Glory tokens (stars) and a scoring cube per player
- Coins (money)
- Solo mode (Automa) components

*Icons on the components are written out as words in this rulebook: Power, Guile, corruption, worker, map, coin/money, card.*

## Setup

1. **Build the map.** Assemble the shared map of 20 hex locations in its three zones. The **Southern** locations are placed **face up** (three of them sit adjacent to the Basecamp board). The **Central** and **Northern** locations start **face down**, each covered by a **map token**. Place a few cards from the main deck into the gaps around the map, face up, so they can be gathered during play.
2. **Prepare corruption.** Place the corruption tokens in the supply/bag. Corruption covers the benefits on many of the more distant, more dangerous locations; higher-value corruption guards the richer rewards. The single high-value corruption tile is set on its dedicated location.
3. **Set out the Basecamp board** where all players can reach it. It displays the glory categories and the glory track.
4. **Give each player** a mech in their color placed in a matching base, a mech mat, a matching scoring cube, and their four glory tokens. Each player also takes a **character card and its matching companion card** to form a starting hand, and places their mech at the starting Basecamp area of the map.
5. Determine a first player. Play proceeds clockwise.

## Playing the Game

Turns pass one at a time around the table. **On your turn you take a single action**, choosing one of:

- **Move** your mech, or
- **Play** a card, or
- **Gather** at your mech's location, or
- **Refresh** (recover your cards and workers).

Because you take only one action per turn, Expeditions rewards planning ahead: you build up cards and Power/Guile over several turns, spend them, then Refresh to get everything back. Some cards and abilities grant extra actions or let you combine effects, but the default is one action per turn.

### The Play action

Play one card from your hand into your **active area** (the tableau to the side of your mech mat). When you play a card you immediately gain the resource shown on it — either **Power** or **Guile** — and you may use the card's instant benefit. While a card sits in your active area it provides an ongoing ability, and it offers a **worker ability** that you can trigger later by placing one of your workers on the card. Cards you have played stay in your active area (and their workers stay committed) until you Refresh.

### The Move action

Move your mech from its current location toward a destination up to your **Move range** away. Your default Move range is **1-3 hexes**; certain items and upgrades increase it. Only one mech may occupy a location at a time, so mechs can temporarily block one another.

When your mech reaches a **face-down** location, flip it (explore it): reveal its benefit and take the **map token** that was covering it into your supply. Map tokens are useful in their own right and count toward a glory category. Many explored locations are covered by **corruption**, which must be dealt with before their benefit can be used (see Corruption and Vanquishing).

### The Gather action

Gather takes the **benefit shown on the location your mech is standing on**. As part of a Gather you may optionally place a worker on the benefit to enhance or repeat it. Location benefits vary across the map and can include:

- **Gain a card** — take a face-up card into your active area (or your hand), building your engine.
- **Gain a worker** — take a worker of the indicated color into your supply.
- **Solve a quest** — complete a quest under your control for its reward (see Quests).
- **Upgrade an item** — improve one of your item cards (see Items).
- **Meld a meteorite** — tuck a meteorite for its cascading bonus (see Meteorites and Melding).
- **Boast** — place a glory token, if you meet a glory category (see Glory).

### The Refresh action

Refreshing is your entire turn. Return **all of the cards from your active area back to your hand**, and return **all of your placed workers to your supply**, making them available again. Refresh is how you recycle a spent engine so you can play the same powerful cards and workers again. (Some abilities let you also take an action on a Refresh turn, but by default Refresh only recovers your cards and workers.)

## Power, Guile, and Vanquishing Corruption

**Power** and **Guile** are the two resources you generate by playing cards; each card shows which one it gives. They are spent to remove corruption.

Corruption tokens each show a **value** and a **color** matching either Power or Guile. To **vanquish** a piece of corruption you use a card ability that allows vanquishing, then **pay the matching resource equal to the token's value** (Power for Power-colored corruption, Guile for Guile-colored corruption). For example, a 4-value Power corruption token costs 4 Power to vanquish. Removing the corruption that covers a location's benefit lets you use that benefit; the corruption tokens you claim are placed on your mech mat, where they are worth money at the end of the game and count toward glory. A single high-value (20) corruption tile sits on the map as a special challenge and its own glory category.

## Building Your Engine

### Workers

Workers are activated by placing them on cards in your active area (or on location benefits during a Gather) to trigger extra effects. Workers stay committed until you Refresh, so you must balance spending them now against holding them for a bigger turn. Workers you hold in your supply also count toward a glory category.

### Quests

Quest cards can come under your control as you explore and gather. When a location or ability lets you **Solve** a quest, you complete it to gain its reward. Solving quests contributes end-game value and counts toward the "solve four quests" glory category. By default each of the repeatable categories (solving quests, upgrading items, melding meteorites) can be done up to **four times** each.

### Items

Item cards provide ongoing benefits. When a benefit lets you **Upgrade** an item, you improve it (typically flipping or enhancing the card) for a stronger ongoing effect and additional end-game money value shown on the card. Upgrading four items is one of the glory categories.

### Meteorites and Melding

Meteorite cards are melded rather than left in your active area. When you **Meld** a meteorite you tuck it under your mech mat and take the meld bonus printed on that card — **and you also re-trigger all of your previously earned meld bonuses**. Because each meld replays every earlier meld, melding builds an accelerating cascade of rewards. Melding four meteorites is a glory category.

## Glory

Glory represents your crew's greatest boasts. Certain locations have a **Boast** benefit; when you Gather on such a location you may place one of your glory tokens on the Basecamp board, provided you currently satisfy one of the glory categories. You may place at most one glory token per Boast benefit, and each category can hold your token only once.

The glory categories (ways to earn a glory token) are:

1. **Solve four quests.**
2. **Meld four meteorites.**
3. **Upgrade four items.**
4. **Vanquish seven corruption** (across all the corruption tokens you have claimed).
5. **Vanquish the lone high-value (20) corruption tile.**
6. **Have eight cards** in your hand and active area combined.
7. **Have seven workers, or five map tokens**, in your supply.

Each player has four glory tokens to place over the game.

## End of the Game

The end of the game is triggered the moment a player **places their fourth glory token** on the Basecamp board. That player finishes their turn, then **each other player takes one final turn**. The game then ends and players score.

## Scoring

All scoring is in **money (coins)**; whoever has the most money wins. Sources of end-game money typically include:

- **Coins** you have accumulated during the game (money is worth its face value, 1 point each).
- **Glory tokens** you placed, each worth the value shown for its category on the Basecamp board (more demanding achievements are worth more).
- **Claimed corruption** you removed and stored on your mat, each worth money.
- **Upgraded items**, worth the money value printed on the upgraded side of each card.
- **Solved quests** and other card effects that grant end-game value.

Total each player's money. The player with the greatest total wins the expedition. Ties are broken in favor of the player who is further along the shared glory track / triggered fewer resources, per the tiebreak on the components.

## Solo Mode

Expeditions includes a solo **Automa** mode in which you compete against an automated opponent driven by a deck of decision cards and a set of progress cards. The Automa races you to place its glory tokens and claim map rewards, following its own simplified rules while you play by the standard rules above. Consult the dedicated Automa rulebook for the exact card-by-card procedure and difficulty levels.
